# Returns Policy

Effective 2026-01-01. Owner: Customer Trust. Applies to storefront purchases.

## Return window
ShopCore accepts returns within 30 days of delivery for new items in original packaging with receipt or order number.

## Opened items
Opened items can be returned for any reason within 14 days of delivery. Perishable goods are non-returnable once opened.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of the returned item passing warehouse inspection. Refunds above $50 require manager approval; refunds above $500 require finance lead approval.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, downloadable software licenses once activated, and custom-built PCs purchased through the configurator cannot be returned.

## Exchanges
Size and color exchanges for apparel are free within 30 days. Other exchanges are treated as a return plus a new purchase.
