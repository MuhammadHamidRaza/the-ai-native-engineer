# Price Adjustment Policy

Effective 2026-01-01. Owner: Pricing.

## Price drops after purchase
If an item you purchased drops in price on our storefront within 14 days of your order date, contact support with your order number and we will refund the difference.

## Exclusions
Flash sales opening within 24 hours of purchase, bundle pricing, and prices matched during checkout promotions are excluded. Clearance and final-sale items are excluded.

## How long it takes
Approved adjustments are credited to the original payment method within 3 business days.
