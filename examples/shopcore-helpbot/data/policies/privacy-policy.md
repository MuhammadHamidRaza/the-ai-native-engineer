# Privacy Policy Summary

Effective 2026-01-01. Owner: Legal.

## Data we store
Account email, name, addresses, order history, and support conversations. Card data is tokenized by the payment processor; ShopCore never sees full card numbers.

## What reaches AI assistants
Only policy documents and order status relevant to the current conversation. Never full payment details, never passwords, never government IDs.

## Retention
Order records are kept 7 years (tax law). Support chats are kept 24 months hot, then anonymized.

## Your rights
You can request a copy or deletion of your personal data by writing to privacy@shopcore.example. Deletion cascades to support chats and marketing lists within 30 days, except records required by law.

## Sharing
We share data with the payment processor and the delivery carrier, nothing else. We never sell personal data.
