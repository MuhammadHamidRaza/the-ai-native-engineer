# Shipping Policy

Effective 2026-01-01. Owner: Logistics.

## Delivery estimates
Standard shipping is 3-5 business days. Express is 1-2 business days. Estimates start when the warehouse confirms picking, not at checkout.

## Shipping costs
Orders over $75 ship free (standard). Express is always $12. Orders under $75 pay $4.99 standard.

## Late delivery
If a standard order arrives more than 10 business days late, contact support with order number; we will refund the shipping fee.

## Damaged on arrival
Photograph the package and item within 48 hours. We will ship a replacement or issue a refund for the item price plus original shipping.

## Restricted items
Lithium batteries ship regulated: ground transport only, no express to PO boxes.
