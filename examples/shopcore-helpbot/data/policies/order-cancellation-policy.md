# Order Cancellation Policy

Effective 2026-01-01. Owner: Customer Trust.

## Self-service cancellation
You can cancel any order that has not yet entered the picking stage from your account's order detail page. The button disappears once picking starts.

## After picking starts
If picking already started, ask support chat. Cancellation at this stage is discretionary: we may substitute it with a return-ship-on-arrival flow.

## Refunds on cancellation
Cancelled-before-shipping orders are refunded to the original payment method within 2 business days.

## Unpaid orders
Orders in 'pending' status for over 72 hours are automatically cancelled and stock is released.
