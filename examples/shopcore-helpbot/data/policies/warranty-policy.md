# Warranty Policy

Effective 2026-01-01. Owner: Product Quality.

## Coverage
Electronics carry a 12-month hardware warranty from delivery date. Keyboard, mouse and cables carry 24 months. Software licenses are covered for installation defects for 90 days.

## Claims
File a warranty claim with order number and photos of the defect. Assessment takes 3 business days. Replacement, repair or refund is chosen by ShopCore; refunds are capped at purchase price.

## Exclusions
Physical damage, liquid damage, and third-party modifications void the warranty. Commercial/high-wear use beyond designed duty cycles is excluded.

## Battery capacity
Batteries are consumables: 70% of rated capacity after 500 cycles is not a defect.
