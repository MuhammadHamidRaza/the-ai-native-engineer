This section is the craft at the center of Postgres operations: **decide what to build (24.1.1), then read what the planner actually did (24.1.2).** The two lessons are not separable in practice — indexes are hypotheses, plans are the experiment.

## What Is In This Section

- **24.1.1 B-Tree, GIN and Composite Indexes** — how a B-Tree actually works (sorted 8 kB pages, three levels deep at millions of rows, the leftmost-prefix rule); composite column order via **Equality → Sort → Range** (the ESR rule from [12.2.3](/book/part-04-databases-backend-and-full-stack-development/12-sql-postgresql-and-mongodb/12-2-sql-and-relational-querying/12-2-3-indexes-and-explain-analyze), sharpened for range columns); covering indexes with `INCLUDE` and why they unlock index-only scans; partial indexes (`WHERE status = 'pending'`) for the hot warehouse screen; GIN for JSONB containment, full-text search and tag arrays; BRIN for append-mostly `orders.created_at`; index bloat and `REINDEX CONCURRENTLY`; `pg_stat_user_indexes` for finding dead weight; and when indexing is the *wrong* answer (write-heavy tables, boolean columns, tiny tables) — always verified with `EXPLAIN ANALYZE`.

- **24.1.2 Reading EXPLAIN ANALYZE Plans** — the anatomy of `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)`: `Seq Scan` vs `Index Scan` vs `Bitmap Index Scan`, `Nested Loop` vs `Hash Join`, `Sort`/`Materialize`, `loops`, `Rows Removed by Filter`, actual vs estimated rows (and the row-underestimation semantics that produce catastrophic plans); four real ShopCore plans dissected before/after with typical — not guaranteed — timing characterisation; `auto_explain` for production sampling; `pg_stat_statements` for finding the query you forgot you had.

## How the Two Lessons Fit

```text
  24.1.1 (build a hypothesis)              24.1.2 (test the hypothesis)
  --------------------------              ---------------------------------
  orders(user_id, status, created_at)  ->   EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)
  WHERE status='pending' partial index ->   Seq Scan 84 ms -> Index Scan 0.6 ms
  GIN on products.search_tsv           ->   Bitmap Heap Scan, Buffers: shared hit=...
            |                                        |
            +------------ both end in proof ----------+
                     pg_stat_user_indexes / pg_stat_statements
```

The rule that governs both: **no index ships without its plan attached.**

## Exit for This Section

You are done with 24.1 when the four hottest ShopCore queries each have: an index you can justify column-by-column, an `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` before/after you captured yourself, and a `pg_stat_statements` entry showing its share of total database time dropping.
