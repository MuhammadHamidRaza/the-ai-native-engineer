A worker that is "an agent with a name" will drift: it will answer questions nobody asked, spend money nobody capped, and email customers nobody approved. This section is the design phase — the discipline that keeps a role-specific worker bounded, measurable and killable, before you build the first node.

## The Worker Design Spec — One Page, No Exceptions

Every worker starts as a one-page spec, written before any automation exists:

```text
WORKER SPEC: AR Nudge v1.2
Mission:        Recover overdue B2B invoices with polite, escalating reminders.
Inputs:         invoices.due_date, customer contact, invoice amount, prior touches
Outputs:        Email drafts (approval-gated), CRM timeline notes, Slack alerts
Deterministic guardrails:
                - Never email a customer whose invoice has a dispute flag
                - Max 4 touches; never > 1 touch per 7 days
                - Amount > €10,000 → human-only, no automation
                - Tone ladder enforced by template id: L1 (friendly) → L4 (final)
LLM scope:      Personalize tone only; template and facts are NOT LLM-editable
Budget:         ≤ €60/mo tokens + Twilio €0.02/SMS; hard stop at €80
SLA:            Runs daily 08:00 CET; nudge sent within 1h of trigger; 99% on-time
Kill-switch:    Feature flag WORKERS_AR=1 → 0; owner notified on trip
Owner:          RevOps lead (A. Rahman); backup: Finance manager
Escalation:     Replies citing dispute/lawyer/hardship → human queue, same-day
Audit:          worker_runs row per invoice per touch (input hash, template, approver)
```

If you cannot fill in every line, the worker is not ready to build — and "the LLM will handle it" is the tell.

## Why Design Comes Before Building

The failure mode of AI workers is never "the model was too weak" — it is "nobody decided what the worker may not do." Boundaries are cheap on paper and ruinous in production. A one-page spec reviewed by the business owner (not just engineering) is the cheapest governance artifact you will ever write.

## Section Exit Criteria

- You can write a complete worker spec for Inbox Triage, AR Nudge and Research Brief from memory of the template above.
- You can defend, for each worker, why it is event-driven vs scheduled — and what it does when its dependency is down.
- You can implement a kill-switch that a non-engineer can operate.
