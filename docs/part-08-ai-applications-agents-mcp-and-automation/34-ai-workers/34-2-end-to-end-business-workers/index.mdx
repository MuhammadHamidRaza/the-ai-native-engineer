Specs and triggers are not a business outcome. This section builds the three ShopCore workers end-to-end — the Inbox Triage flow with policy-grounded draft replies and escalation, the AR Nudge tone ladder with stop conditions, and the weekly Research Brief with citations — then closes with the audit trail and escalation queue that make these workers safe to employ.

## The Three Workers at a Glance

| Worker | Trigger | Brain | Side effect (gated) |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Inbox Triage | New support email (Gmail Trigger) | LLM classify + Ch 28 RAG draft | Draft reply → approval → send; else Linear ticket |
| AR Nudge | Daily 08:00 cron | Template ladder L1→L4 + light personalization | Email/SMS draft → approval → send |
| Research Brief | Weekly cron (Mon 07:00) | Multi-source retrieval + synthesis | Notion page with citations → internal review |

Common skeleton for all three: **intake → enrich (RAG/DB) → decide (LLM, structured) → gate (policy/approval) → act → audit**. Build this skeleton once in n8n; each worker is a specialization of it.

## Why Audit Trails Are in Scope Here

A worker you cannot audit is a liability with an API key. When a customer disputes a nudge ("your bot hounded me about a paid invoice") or Legal wants every AI-touched communication, the answer must be a query — not archaeology. The pattern from Chapter 36 (production telemetry) starts here, small and concrete.

## Section Exit Criteria

- One complete Inbox Triage run visible in: n8n Executions → draft reply in Slack → approver click → sent mail → `worker_runs` row.
- AR Nudge refuses to touch a dispute-flagged invoice — with the skip reason visible in the log.
- Research Brief produces a Notion page where every claim carries a URL and retrieval date.
- Escalation queue reviewed daily; DLQ empty or triaged; you can answer "what did the workers do last Tuesday?" with one SQL query.
