**Chapter 25 (`16 Focused Hours`)** has two halves that every professional needs in full — using AI for ordinary work, and understanding the models that make it possible.

## Chapter 25 Structure

- **25.1 AI Assistants for Everyday Work:** The honest boundary between conversational assistants and AI coding assistants; choosing ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini by job; complete workflows for email, meetings, reports, research, translation, documents, PDFs, spreadsheets and presentations; privacy, verification and hallucination discipline.
- **25.2 How Language Models Work & Programmatic Access:** Tokens, context windows, sampling; model capability/cost/latency trade-offs; structured outputs and JSON Schemas; streaming, tool calling and multimodal inputs.

## What You Will Be Able to Do

- Draft a week's worth of professional writing in an hour — and catch the one sentence that would embarrass you in front of legal.
- Turn 30 pages of a contract into the three questions your lawyer needs to answer, and verify every claim you carry into the room.
- Decide when an API call beats a chatbot and wire the difference into your own applications with schemas and streaming.

Prerequisites: Parts 01–04. No prior ML knowledge assumed — we go from "what is a token" up.
