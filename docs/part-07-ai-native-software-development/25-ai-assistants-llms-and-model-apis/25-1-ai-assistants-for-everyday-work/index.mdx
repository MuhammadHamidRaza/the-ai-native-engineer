Every professional now has a capable general-purpose AI assistant within arm's reach — in a browser tab, a phone, and increasingly inside the apps they already use. This section teaches you to use those assistants deliberately: picking the right one for the job, running real workflows end to end, and not embarrassing yourself or your organization with plausible-looking nonsense.

This is not a hype tour and not a list of "prompts that will replace you." It is the practical layer between "I opened ChatGPT once" and "I operate an assistant fluently, verify its output, and know exactly when it should not be trusted."

## What This Section Covers

- **25.1.1 The Category Map** — conversational assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, Mistral, DeepSeek) versus AI coding assistants (Claude Code, Codex CLI, Cursor, Copilot), and the failure modes of using the wrong one.
- **25.1.2 Choosing by Job** — writers, researchers, developers, analysts, designers, PMs and founders pick differently; current plans and approximate prices (verified November 2026), with links to official pricing pages.
- **25.1.3 Everyday Workflows** — five complete workflows with real prompts and real verification: status emails, meetings, competitor research, translation, executive summaries.
- **25.1.4 Documents, PDFs, Spreadsheets and Presentations** — the actual click-paths, worked document-Q&A tasks, spreadsheet formula help, and the honest limits of a 200-page PDF.
- **25.1.5 Privacy, Verification and Hallucination Prevention** — what never to paste, enterprise data controls, how to reproduce and catch hallucinations, and a pre-flight checklist for anything leaving your desk.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this section you will be able to:

- Classify any AI tool you encounter into the correct category and predict how it fails.
- Pick ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini (or name the honest alternative) for a specific job class — and say why.
- Run the five core workflows in 25.1.3 unassisted, with verification built in, not bolted on.
- Upload a document, interrogate it, and catch the two ways the answer can be wrong.
- State — without looking it up — what you may and may not paste into a free consumer tier.
- Apply the claims-inventory → source-check → contradiction-test discipline to any AI output before it reaches a client.

## The Honest Hours Budget

Budget **6–7 focused hours** for this section (Chapter 25 as a whole is 16). You can skim it in an afternoon; you cannot absorb it in an afternoon. The verification habit — the single most transferable skill here — only sticks if you practice it on your own real work, not on the toy examples. Plan at least two hours of hands-on practice inside your own email drafts, your own PDFs, and your own spreadsheets.

Prerequisites: Parts 01–04. No machine-learning background is required — that is what Section 25.2 is for. You do need a working account on at least one of ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini (all three have free tiers), a browser, and a willingness to look foolish in private so you never do it in public.

## What You Will Be Able to Do on Day 1

- Draft a weekly status update in five minutes instead of thirty — and catch the invented metric before your boss does.
- Turn a two-hour meeting's worth of notes into decisions and action items, with owners.
- Answer "what does this contract say about termination?" from a 30-page PDF in ten minutes — with page references you verify yourself.
- Refuse, politely and specifically, when a colleague asks you to "just paste the client list into the AI."
- Know which $20/month subscription is the right one for your job and why — and when the free tier is enough.

## A Note on Names and Prices

AI product names, model names and prices change every few months. Every price and model name in this section was verified against official pages in **November 2026**; each page links its sources. Where a claim could not be verified at writing time it is marked **CLAIM TO VERIFY** rather than presented as fact. Treat numbers as honest approximations, not as a quote you can budget a procurement order around — always re-check the linked official page before paying.
