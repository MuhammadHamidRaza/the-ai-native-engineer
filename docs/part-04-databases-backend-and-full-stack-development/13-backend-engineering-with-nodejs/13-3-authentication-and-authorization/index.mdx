**Subchapter 13.3** covers who the user is and what they may do: password hashing and sessions vs JWTs, logout and protected routes, role-based access control that stops IDOR attacks, and OAuth 2.0 so your ShopCore users can sign in with GitHub.

## Lessons in Subchapter 13.3

1. **[13.3.1 Sessions, JWTs and Password Hashing](/book/part-04-databases-backend-and-full-stack-development/13-backend-engineering-with-nodejs/13-3-authentication-and-authorization/13-3-1-sessions-jwts-and-password-hashing):** `scrypt`/`argon2` hashing, `HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite` cookies, when JWTs beat sessions — and vice versa.
2. **[13.3.2 Role-Based Access Control and Resource Ownership](/book/part-04-databases-backend-and-full-stack-development/13-backend-engineering-with-nodejs/13-3-authentication-and-authorization/13-3-2-role-based-access-control-and-ownership):** `requireRole`, ownership-scoped SQL (`WHERE id = $1 AND user_id = $2`), and why frontend-only role checks are not security.
3. **[13.3.3 OAuth 2.0: Sign in with GitHub](/book/part-04-databases-backend-and-full-stack-development/13-backend-engineering-with-nodejs/13-3-authentication-and-authorization/13-3-3-oauth-2-0-sign-in-with-github):** Create the OAuth app, walk the authorization-code + PKCE flow, and mint your own session afterward.
