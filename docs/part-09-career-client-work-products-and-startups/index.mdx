Every layer of this book so far taught you to build. Part 09 teaches you to **get paid for it** — and to do the work in professional settings where communication, judgment and reliability matter as much as code.

Part 09 is where engineers stop being interchangeable hands and become people organizations fund, clients trust, and founders want on the inside. It is also where a lot of talented engineers make avoidable mistakes: resumes that hide their evidence, outreach that sounds like spam, freelancing that collapses into feast-or-famine, and MVPs that were built before the problem was understood.

## Who This Part Is For

- Engineers targeting **permanent roles** — start at Chapter 37, then 38.
- Engineers considering **freelance or independent consulting** — 37 → 39 in order, then Appendix A–C alongside.
- Engineers with a **product idea** — 40 → 41, after skimming 39 for how to get first customers.
- **Everyone** — 37 applies regardless of path: you will work in a team.

## What You Will Produce

| A launch plan with one distribution channel chosen and a unit-economics model | 41 |

## How Part 09 Connects to the Rest of the Book

- **Parts 00–08** built the engineering competence the market pays for. Part 09 converts it into career capital — the same skills, aimed at outcomes.
- **Part 10** takes the strongest product candidate and turns it into the capstone.
- **Appendices A–C** are the operational reference shelf for Chapter 39 (playbook, templates, workbook). **Appendix D** supports Chapter 38. **Appendix E** is the all-purpose reference.

Part 09 assumes you can build. It does not assume you know how to sell, position, negotiate, or operate in a team — that is what it teaches.
