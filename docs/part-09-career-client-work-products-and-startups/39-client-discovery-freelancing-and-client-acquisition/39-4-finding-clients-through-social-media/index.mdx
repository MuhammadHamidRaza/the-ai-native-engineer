Twelve platforms, one playbook: **identify buyers → provide visible value → invite to a low-stakes conversation**. The platform changes; the mechanics don't. This subchapter is deliberately not twelve shallow "how to post" guides — it's a decision system for picking 2–3 channels that match your niche and executing them with discipline, plus genuinely useful platform-specific differences.

## How to Choose

Pick **LinkedIn + one other** matched to your niche — e.g., LinkedIn + Discord (founder/technical), LinkedIn + Facebook Groups (local trades), LinkedIn + YouTube (authority). Master those before adding a third.

## The Shared Cross-Platform Playbook

1. **Profile = landing page** — buyer lands on your profile in 3s and decides.
2. **Value before ask** — 80% content/help, 20% outreach.
3. **One ask** — "Worth 15 minutes?" — never "hire me".
4. **Track everything** — a spreadsheet of touches, replies, calls, proposals.
5. **Consistency beats bursts** — 20 min/day outperforms 4h once a month.
