TikTok is a discovery engine with one unusual property: it can put a stranger in front of millions without any audience history, then forget your name five minutes later. For freelance acquisition it rarely produces a $40k architecture contract — it reliably produces a $2k restaurant-website inquiry on Tuesday if you're the one explaining online ordering to non-technical owners in 45 seconds. It rewards one thing — holding attention — and delivers whoever holds it, regardless of credentials.

## 1. What TikTok Is Good For (and Not Good For) in 2026

Good for: local-business and consumer-adjacent tech services, personal-brand presence, top-of-funnel awareness, humanizing the engineer (owners hire the face they recognize), productized offers explainable simply. Not good for: enterprise B2B, deep architecture consulting, long sales cycles, buyers over ~45 who distrust the app, or dense specification offers. Realistic expectation for B2B technical services: a supporting channel, never the spine of your pipeline.

## 2. Who Hires There and at What Deal Size

| Buyer | Typical deal size | What they buy |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Restaurants / cafés / salons | $800–$4k | Online ordering, booking, Google profile |
| Boutique e-commerce | $1k–$8k | Store builds, product pages |
| Coaches / creators | $1k–$5k retainers | Funnels, landing pages |
| Local home services | $500–$3k | Lead-gen sites, review automation |
| Aspiring devs (education products only) | n/a | (relevant only if you also sell courses) |

Practitioner ranges. Deal flow is impulsive — a video performs Monday, inquiries land Monday night, deals close within days.

## 3. Profile Setup That Matters

- **Business vs Creator account:** Business gives analytics, link placement, and (region-dependent) contact buttons; Creator gives trending audio and more music options. Pick Business if you want the "email me" button to work one-tap.
- **Username:** name + niche (@hamidbuildsweb). **Name field** carries search weight — use "Hamid | Websites for Restaurants".
- **Bio:** one line: who + outcome + proof + CTA. Example: "Websites & online ordering for restaurants · 25+ launches · DM 'ORDER' for a free menu-page audit".
- **Link:** one clickable link (bio). Point to a booking page or audit landing page, not your homepage.
- **Pinned videos (3):** before/after case study, a 30-second "what I charge and why", a client testimonial clip (with permission).

## 4. Reach Mechanics

TikTok distributes by interest graph, not social graph: videos are tested on small cohorts and expanded if retention holds. The first 1–2 seconds decide everything — open on the outcome ("I rebuilt this restaurant's ordering page and they make 30% more per order"), not "Hey guys." Completion rate and replays matter more than likes; 15–45s, one idea, outperform rambling videos. Captions are mandatory. Posting 1–2×/day for the first 60 days is the reliable exit from zero-view purgatory.

## 5. The Workflow: Video → Profile → DM → Booking

1. Identify one local/technical pain per video ("why Wix ordering loses 20% of carts", "the menu PDF is killing your mobile conversions").
2. Record the screen + face, narrate the fix in plain language.
3. Close with a spoken CTA: "DM 'ORDER' and I'll audit your menu page free."
4. Reply to every comment and DM the same day — speed closes local deals.
5. In DM: ask 2 qualifying questions (business type, timeline), then send one fixed-scope option with price.

Worked example:

```text
Video hook (spoken, first 2s): "This restaurant was losing every phone
order on Saturdays — here's the $900 fix."

Comment reply → DM: "Hey, saw your comment — what's your current menu
setup (PDF, link, delivery apps?). If it's a PDF, I rebuild one-page
mobile ordering for restaurants, fixed $900, live in a week. Want the
layout mockup first, free?"
```

## 6. Format Specifics

- **Length:** 15–45s default; 60–90s for walkthroughs.
- **Vertical 9:16, face + screen, captions burned in.** Video beats static posts ~always on TikTok.
- **One idea per video.** Series work well ("Restaurant fixing Wednesdays" #1–6) for repeat exposure.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5, mix niche + local (#restauranttok #dallasbusiness #shopify). No generic #fyp stuffing.
- **Links:** bio only; captions aren't clickable.

## 7. Limits, Risks, Bans, Scams

Repetitive hashtags, mass-DMing, or copied videos trigger shadowban/restriction. Keep identical-DM volume low; personalize. Fake client scams mirror Instagram: overpayment checks, "marketing manager" jobs paying in crypto, requests to buy domains/gift cards — never. Paid "promotion" services offering views are fake engagement; avoid. Payment protection doesn't exist on-platform — get deposits before work. Content rules are regional; avoid income claims ("I made $50k/mo") with no proof, it violates policies and reads as scam. Keep client names/data blurred unless they gave written consent.

## 8. KPIs and Weekly Cadence

| KPI | Practitioner range | Cadence |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Videos posted | 5–10 | weekly |
| Avg watch completion | track % | per video |
| DMs opened | 3–15 | weekly |
| Calls booked | 1–4 | weekly |
| Follower growth | steady | monthly |

Cadence: batch record 5–7 videos in one hour weekly; 15 min/day on comments/DMs. Track DMs → calls → closes; views without DMs mean CTA or offer is unclear.

## 9. When NOT to Bother

Skip TikTok if your buyers are CTOs, procurement teams, or engineers; if you cannot post consistently for 60+ days; if your offer can't be shown visually or explained simply; or if you are uncomfortable with public video of your face and voice. For B2B technical pipelines, spend the same hour on LinkedIn or YouTube.

## 10. Exercise

1. Pick one client type; shoot 3 videos on one recurring pain, different hooks.
2. Bio: keyword name, outcome line, CTA keyword; pin your best mockup.
3. Post 5 videos in 7 days; reply to all comments within 24h.
4. Track which hook drives the most profile visits; iterate.

**Acceptance criteria.** Three videos published with caption hooks in the first 2 seconds; bio and pinned content complete. Posting streak of 5 videos with daily comment engagement. Within 2 weeks: at least 10 qualified DMs opened or 2 booked calls from local prospects; if views are high but DMs zero, fix the spoken CTA and bio link, not the niche.
