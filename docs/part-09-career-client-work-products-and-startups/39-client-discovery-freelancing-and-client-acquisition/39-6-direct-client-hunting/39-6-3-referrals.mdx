One warm introduction beats fifty cold emails. Referrals convert at 25–40% versus &lt;10% for cold — and the clients come pre-trusting, pre-budgeting, pre-aligned. This lesson teaches how engineers manufacture introductions without ever saying "do you know anyone who needs work?"

## 1. The Referral Stack

1. **Client referrals** — your current clients' peers (highest trust, easiest gold).
2. **Ex-colleagues** — peers who moved companies and need your niche skill.
3. **Vendors/partners** — agencies who can't do your niche; they refer for a fee.
4. **Community/OSS** — contributors, maintainers, OpenAI Discord people — warm by default.
5. **Personal network** — friends who own businesses; say yes to a 20-min "quick ask".

Rank your existing network honestly: who already trusts you and has buyers?

## 2. The Referral Ask, Done Right

Never: "Do you know anyone who needs a developer?"
Always: "Priya, I saw a postmortem from ShopCore. If someone there struggles with Stripe webhooks, I'd appreciate an intro — not asking you to vouch for me, just open the door."

Principles: **specific person, specific problem, specific role, specific ask**. The ask is the intro, not the job. If they want to check your fit, one short call with the candidate solves everything.

## 3. Making Your Clients Referrers

- **Deliver visibly** — weekly report + wins. They talk to peers; they need material.
- **Give before you receive** — intros from your network outward; reciprocate first.
- **Ask at peak satisfaction** — the moment after a launch, a rescue, or a painful fix.
- **Make it trivially easy** — draft the forwardable intro email for them; one line to edit.

## 4. The Referral Flywheel

One strong client → 1–2 referrals → 1 becomes a project → onward chain. Track referred vs direct vs inbound; double down on whatever compounds.

## 5. Common Failure Modes

- **Vague asks** — "any companies you know?" = zero referrals; be specific.
- **Asking too early** — before any delivery; you earn the referral with proof.
- **No follow-through** — if someone intros you, report back the outcome within 48h.
- **Forgetting to thank** — a handwritten note/gift for a referrer keeps the tap open.

## 6. Exercise

List 10 ex-colleagues/clients. Message each with a specific 3-line ask tailored to an opportunity in their world. Follow up after a week. Log responses.

**Acceptance criteria.** Your log shows 10 sends, ≥2 intro calls booked (or an honest "network is cold — plan to warm it" reflection).
