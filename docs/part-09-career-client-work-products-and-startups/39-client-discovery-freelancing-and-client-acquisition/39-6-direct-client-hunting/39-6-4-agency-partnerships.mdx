Agencies have clients but not every skill in-house — and you have a skill but not their pipeline. The agency partnership is the highest-leverage distribution channel for technical contractors: you plug in as their overflow or white-label engineer, pre-vetted, pre-positioned.

## 1. The Three Partnership Models

| Model | How it works | Fit |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Overflow engineer** | Agency sells, you deliver overflow work | Steady flow once trusted |
| **White-label subcontractor** | Agency sells as theirs, you deliver invisibly | Highest volume, lowest visibility |
| **Referral partner** | Agency introduces, you close & deliver | Highest margin, lowest dependency |

## 2. What Agencies Actually Need

1. **Reliability** — they risk their reputation; flaky contractors get dropped first.
2. **Speed** — 48h diagnosis beats 5-day perfect.
3. **Communication** — daily Slack/standup clarity beats brilliance.
4. **Domain overlap** — niche match (Shopify, Salesforce, healthcare, finance).

## 3. Opening the Conversation

```text
Hi [agency], I'm Hamid — solo engineer doing [niche: AI deflection /
Shopify rescue / billing migrations]. You clearly have more inbound client
demand than your core team covers — I plug in as delivery overflow for
[your niche] projects, fully white-labeled if preferred, with clear SLAs.
Suggesting a 15-min intro; if the fit's wrong, no harm done.
```

## 4. Structure the Deal

Rate split: agency bills $120/h, you receive $80-90/h (subcontractor). Or referral: agency bills their rate, you bill client directly, agency takes 10–20% for the intro. Put it in a 1-page agreement: scope, rate, payment terms (net-15/30), IP assignment, liability cap.

## 5. Common Failure Modes

- **Undercutting the agency's rate** — you're the risk they inherit; respect the margin.
- **Overpromising capacity** — say no when full; one missed deadline burns the channel.
- **No written agreement** — verbal deals evaporate at the first tense invoice.
- **Ignoring communication norms** — agency status reports to clients need your input on time.

## 6. Exercise

List 5 agencies in your niche (search "[niche] agency" or check Clutch/UpCity). Send 3 partnership pitches. Track responses.

**Acceptance criteria.** Your pitch includes: the agency's specific service gap you'd fill, your niche proof, and a 15-min locked-in call request. ≥1 replied or you know why (no fit, or pitch unclear).
