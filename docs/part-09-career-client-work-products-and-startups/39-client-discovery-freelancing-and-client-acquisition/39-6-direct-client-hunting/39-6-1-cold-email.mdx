Cold email is the most-hated and most-effective outbound channel engineers refuse to learn. Done right it produces the highest-value technical contracts of any channel; done wrong, it's spam. The difference is research, relevance, and restraint.

## 1. The Anatomy of an Email That Gets Replies

Subject (5 words max) → who/what (1 line) → specific observation (1 line) → one low-stakes ask → signature. That's the whole email. 80–120 words max.

```text
Subject: Your checkout latency post — one question

Hi Priya — I saw the postmortem on your checkout 500ms regression after the
May deploy. The root cause (double Redis read in middleware) is the exact
pattern I fixed at shopcore.dev with one await cache-lookup-and-memo.

Not pitching — curious how you decided between middleware vs controller-level.

Hamid — full-stack/AI engineer, shopcore.dev
```

Notice: **specific** (mentions their real artifact), **one ask** (curiosity, not money), **no links**, **no attachments**.

## 2. The Research Layer (Before Writing Anything)

- Company engineering blog / postmortems — your hook.
- Job postings — where the pain lives ("hiring platform engineer" = scaling pain).
- Funding/press — new money = budget and urgency.
- Tech stack from Wappalyzer/BuiltWith — name their tools.

**Never** send a message you can't personalize this way. Templated volume is detectable and burns domains.

## 3. Deliverability Hygiene

- Dedicated sending domain (or subdomain) with SPF/DKIM/DMARC set.
- 20–30 sends/day per mailbox max.
- Warm the mailbox 2 weeks before campaigns.
- No links, no images in first-touch.
- Honor opt-out instantly; log every unsubscribe.

## 4. Follow-Up That Doesn't Nag

Sequence: Day 0 → Day 4 → Day 9 → stop. Each touch adds value or brevity:
Day 4: "Bumping in case it got buried — did the May deploy pattern change since?"
Day 9: "No reply needed — leaving this for whenever your Q3 planning happens."

## 5. KPIs

20 personalized emails/day → 20–30% open, 10–15% reply → 3–5 calls/week. Below 5% reply? Rewrite the subject/hook, not more volume.

## 6. Exercise

Write 10 emails to 10 real companies in your niche. Run the filter: can you state the hook out loud in one sentence? If not, the email dies. Send 3, track replies, iterate.

**Acceptance criteria.** Your emails: ≤120 words, one ask, zero links/attachments, personalized hook from their actual public artifact. Track open/reply rates honestly.
