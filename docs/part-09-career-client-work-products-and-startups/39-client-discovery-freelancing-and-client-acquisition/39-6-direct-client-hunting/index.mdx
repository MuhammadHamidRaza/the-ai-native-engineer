Marketplaces give you demand; direct hunting gives you margin, relationship, and repeat business. This subchapter teaches five complementary channels — from highest-trust (referrals) to most scalable (cold email) — and the agency-partnership ladder that multiplies your distribution without building an audience from zero.

## Lessons in Subchapter 39.6

1. **[39.6.1 Cold Email](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/39-client-discovery-freelancing-and-client-acquisition/39-6-direct-client-hunting/39-6-1-cold-email):** The research-relevance-restraint formula, deliverability hygiene, follow-up cadence, and the 20-send daily rule.
2. **[39.6.2 Direct Outreach](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/39-client-discovery-freelancing-and-client-acquisition/39-6-direct-client-hunting/39-6-2-direct-outreach):** The outreach ladder, commenting as stealth warming, the tracked-pipeline discipline.
3. **[39.6.3 Referrals](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/39-client-discovery-freelancing-and-client-acquisition/39-6-direct-client-hunting/39-6-3-referrals):** Referral stack, the specific-ask formula, making clients into referrers, and the flywheel.
4. **[39.6.4 Agency Partnerships](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/39-client-discovery-freelancing-and-client-acquisition/39-6-direct-client-hunting/39-6-4-agency-partnerships):** Three partnership models, what agencies need, and the subcontractor pitch.
5. **[39.6.5 White-Label Services](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/39-client-discovery-freelancing-and-client-acquisition/39-6-direct-client-hunting/39-6-5-white-label-services):** When to go invisible, how to structure the deal, and how to protect yourself.

## How to Use This Subchapter

Run cold email + referrals in parallel. Agencies once you have case-study proof. White-label only when you want volume over brand.
