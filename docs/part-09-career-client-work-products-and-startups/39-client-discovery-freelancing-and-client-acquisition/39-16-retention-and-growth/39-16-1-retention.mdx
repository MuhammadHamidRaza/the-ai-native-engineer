A client you already serve is 5-7× cheaper to grow than a new one to close. Retention turns one-off projects into referral machines — and stable income. This lesson teaches you to become indispensable without becoming a full-time employee again.

## 1. The Retention Playbook

1. **Weekly value touch** — a short win/insight even in quiet weeks.
2. **Quarterly review** — metrics, roadmap, what they should watch.
3. **Proactive ideas** — "I noticed X in your stack; here's a fix most miss." No charge for the tip.
4. **Availability SLA** — guarantee a response window (same-day within business hours).
5. **Expand carefully** — offer the next natural project before asking; frame as "I can see the next win for you."

## 2. Signs You're Losing Them

- They stop responding to status pings.
- They ask for "just this one small thing" with no budget.
- They mention "thinking about alternatives".

Diagnose immediately — often it's unmet expectations, not price.

## 3. The Retainer Design

$2.5k-$5k/mo typical mid-market: fixed hours + on-demand priority + monthly strategy call + quarterly report. Cap scope in writing; renegotiate quarterly.

## 4. Common Failure Modes

- **Disappearing between engagements** — clients churn silently.
- **No expansion path** — you're a vendor, not a partner.
- **Over-availability** — treating them like 9-to-5 employees destroys margin.
- **Only reacting when things break** — invisible work doesn't renew; visible value does.

## 5. A Complete 90-Day Retention Playbook (Brightside Dental)

```text
WEEKLY TOUCH (Friday, 3 min read + 30-sec reply)
"Brightside week 3: 214 billing drafts this week (up 12%), 96% approval,
 Dana's morning review down to 18 min. One flag: 3 drafts cited the old
 policy §4.2 — I repushed the corrected KB; next week should be clean.
 Nothing needed from you."

DAY 30 — 15-min metrics call
- Show: 214 drafts, 96% approval rate, ~7h/week saved (vs baseline)
- Ask: "What almost went wrong this month?" (the only question that matters)
- Note for roadmap: insurance EOB requests 3x expected

DAY 45 — QBR (25 min, slides)
- 30-day outcomes vs kickoff targets (time saved, draft accuracy, patient calls)
- Renewal decision point: annual at $99/mo x 12 (2 months free) or day-60 exit
- Risk check: any silent disengagement? Any thread where they didn't reply?

DAY 90 — Expansion conversation
"You asked about EOBs twice. That's Phase 2: insurance matching + EOB
attach, ~$3,500 one-time or bundle into the $1,900/mo retainer. Want the
one-pager?"
```

**Anatomy:** the Friday note is designed to be 30 seconds of their time and contain **one number they didn't have**; the day-30 call asks the only question that surfaces churn signals early; the QBR turns goodwill into a renewal decision with evidence; the day-90 line turns usage into the next contract — prompted by *their* own words, not yours.

### Retention Cadence Cheatsheet

| Cadence | Length | Content | Goal |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Friday note | 3 min | 1 metric + 1 flag + "nothing needed" | Invisible value made visible |
| Day-30 call | 15 min | "What almost went wrong?" + fix one thing | Catch silent disengagement |
| QBR | 25 min | Baseline vs actuals + renewal ask | Convert goodwill to contract |
| Day-90 expansion | 15 min | Their own repeated ask, priced | Next phase, pre-sold |
