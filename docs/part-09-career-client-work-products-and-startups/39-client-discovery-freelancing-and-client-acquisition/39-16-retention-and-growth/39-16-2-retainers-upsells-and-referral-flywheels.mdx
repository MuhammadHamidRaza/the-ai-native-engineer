Retention without expansion is a treadmill. This lesson teaches the three compounding motions that turn a good project into a referral engine and recurring revenue: structured retainers, intelligent upsells, and referral flywheels that spin without your constant effort.

## 1. Designing a Retainer That Works for Both Sides

| Element | Recommendation |
| :-- | :-- |
| Hours | 20-40/mo fixed |
| Rate | Discounted vs hourly (value of predictability) |
| Scope | Defined buckets; overage at standard rate |
| Term | Month-to-month with 30-day notice |
| Review | Quarterly scope/rate review |

## 2. The Upsell Ladder

Project → retainer → adjacent project → referral. Example: HelpBot pilot → monthly retainer (tuning/evals) → CRM integration when they expand → 2 referrals from their ops lead. Each step deepens the relationship and your position.

## 3. Building a Referral Flywheel

Make referring you the easiest thing their network does: case study write-up they can forward, two sentences on "why us", a referral bonus (credit/gift), quarterly "it’s been a pleasure working with you — know anyone with similar challenges?" — non-pushy, specific.

## 4. Common Failure Modes

- **No referral ask** — clients are happy but never connect you; you must ask.
- **Upsell too early** — sell retention after the win, not before delivery.
- **Referral bonus too transactional** — value-based, personal, specific.

## 5. Exercise

Draft a one-page referral flywheel plan for your ICP: ask script, referral bonus, case-study forwardable link, and quarterly check-in template.

**Acceptance criteria.** Your plan names the exact referral ask, one incentive (if any), and one piece of collateral to forward.
