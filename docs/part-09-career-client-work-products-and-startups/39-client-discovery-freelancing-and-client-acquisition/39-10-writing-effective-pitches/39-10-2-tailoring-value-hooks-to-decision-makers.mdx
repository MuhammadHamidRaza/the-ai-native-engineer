Same product, five inboxes, five pitches. Tailoring your value hook to the actual decision-maker is the difference between "interesting" and "when can you start?" This lesson teaches the hook matrix by stakeholder.

## 1. The Hook Matrix

| Stakeholder | What they hear | What hooks them |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Founder/CEO | "Cost vs value" | Revenue pulled forward, runway, competition |
| VP Eng / CTO | "Risk vs reliability" | Reduced on-call load, better deploys, security |
| Head of Product | "Users vs features" | Faster shipping, fewer regressions, telemetry |
| Head of Ops | "Hours vs errors" | Automation, fewer tickets, cleaner data |
| Head of Sales | "Pipeline vs friction" | Demo-ready assets, account intelligence |

## 2. The Hook Formula

**Persona + Pain + Proof + Ask.** Example (VP Eng):
"Your on-call alerts spiked 4× after the May release. I hardened ShopCore's deploy gate with canary + automated rollback in 2 weeks — 70% fewer pages. Would 15 minutes be useful?"

## 3. The Decision-Maker vs Champion Distinction

Champion = feels the pain, lacks budget authority. Decision-maker = holds the money, may never feel the pain. Your pitch to each differs:
- **Champion** — educate, arm them with internal case studies, make them your internal advocate.
- **Decision-maker** — one-page business case: cost of status quo vs your price.

Don't present technical depth to decision-makers; present business outcomes. Don't present ROI sheets to engineers; present design and reliability.

## 4. Common Failure Modes

- **Same pitch to all levels** — ignored by everyone.
- **Pitching the wrong person first** — the engineer will route you anyway; find the budget holder via LinkedIn/title.
- **Ignoring the champion** — without an internal advocate, deals stall forever.

## 5. Exercise

For one target company, write 3 pitches: Founder, VP Eng, Ops Lead. Identify the champion vs decision-maker and route your outreach accordingly.

**Acceptance criteria.** Each pitch uses the persona's language, one quantified outcome, and a one-click close. Champion identified by name or role.
