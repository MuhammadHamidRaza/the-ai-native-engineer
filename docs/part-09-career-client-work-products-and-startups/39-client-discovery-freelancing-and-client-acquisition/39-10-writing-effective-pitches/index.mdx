The pitch is the bridge between trust and contract. This subchapter teaches you to write short, persona-tailored pitches that get replies — no capability summaries, no decks, just a sharp prediction about their business and one ask.

## Lessons in Subchapter 39.10

1. **[39.10.1 Writing Pitches](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/39-client-discovery-freelancing-and-client-acquisition/39-10-writing-effective-pitches/39-10-1-pitches):** The 3-line prediction + ask, the context→crunch→close call structure, and persona targeting.
2. **[39.10.2 Tailoring Value Hooks to Decision Makers](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/39-client-discovery-freelancing-and-client-acquisition/39-10-writing-effective-pitches/39-10-2-tailoring-value-hooks-to-decision-makers):** The hook matrix by stakeholder, and routing outreach to champion vs decision-maker.

## How to Use This Subchapter

Pick one prospect from your last outbound log; write all three pitches; send the decision-maker version first with the champion as cc.
