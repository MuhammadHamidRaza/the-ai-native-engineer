Comments are the cheapest, highest-leverage visibility tool engineers ignore. A great comment thread can introduce you to 5,000 of your ideal buyers; a bad one, or none, is silence.

## 1. The Anatomy of a Great Comment

1. **Substance** — add data, experience, or a counter-point.
2. **Specificity** — reference their exact words; no "great point".
3. **Warm close** — invite dialogue ("curious how you handled X").

```text
"I hit the same CSP header wall on ShopCore — the Playwright CSP test caught
it only because we ran it in headed mode once. Curious: did you see the same
behavior in headless-only CI?"
```

## 2. Comment Strategy by Platform

- **LinkedIn** — 3-5 comments/week on your ICP's posts; your profile converts commenters to visitors.
- **Reddit** — expertise first, sales never; karma/trust is the currency.
- **Twitter/X** — short, sharp, opinionated takes; quote-tweet with substance.

## 3. Turning Comments into Conversations

The path: like → comment → DM (with context) → 15-min call. Never skip steps; sautéing to the pepper ruins the dish. Comment for 2-3 weeks before DM; the DM then references shared context ("loved your take on CSP — had a quick question").

## 4. Common Failure Modes

- **Spam comments** — "buy my service" kills you instantly.
- **Flattery without substance** — invisible; you're just another like.
- **Argumentative tone** — contrarian is fine; hostile gets blocked.

## 5. Exercise

For one week, post 5 substantive comments on your ideal buyers' posts. Log their reactions; DM the 2 most engaged with a non-pitch resource link.

**Acceptance criteria.** Your log shows ≥5 comments, ≥2 DMs, and ≥1 conversation started without referencing your services.
