A list of 50 perfect prospects beats 5,000 scraped emails. This lesson teaches you to build a targeted, researched, permission-friendly prospect list in an afternoon — and to keep it clean enough to act on.

## 1. The Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) First

Define: industry, size (headcount/revenue), stack, geography, trigger events (funding, hiring, new product, incident), budget signals. Without ICP, lists are spray.

## 2. Sources & Enrichment

- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — filters by headcount/title/funding/seniority; save searches.
- **Apollo.io / Clay** — enrichment + verification; check ToS for scraping ethics.
- **Job postings** — "hiring platform engineer" = your ICP in real-time.
- **Crunchbase/BuiltWith/Wappalyzer** — stack + funding signals.
- **Clutch/UpCity agency listings** — subcontracting channels.
- **Partnerships** — agencies, VCs (portfolio pages), accelerators.

Verify emails (NeverBounce/ZeroBounce); remove bounces immediately.

## 3. List Hygiene Rules

- One row per prospect: name, role, company, trigger, hook, status.
- No purchased lists — they're spam and illegal in GDPR/CCPA contexts.
- Segment by trigger: "freshly funded", "hiring platform", "public outage".
- Refresh weekly; leads decay.

## 4. The 50-Name Rule

Start with 50 researched prospects; personalize 10 outreach messages deeply; measure; iterate. 10 good emails >> 1000 blasted ones.

## 5. Common Failure Modes

- **Quantity over quality** — spraying kills domain reputation.
- **Ignoring triggers** — "new VP of Engineering" beats "general SMB" every time.
- **Stale data** — roles change; verify before sending.

## 6. Exercise

Build a 30-row prospect list for your niche with 3 columns: company, trigger, hook. Verify 10 emails. Draft 3 personalized openers.

**Acceptance criteria.** Your list has ≤30 names, each with a real recent trigger and hook; no unverified emails; no generic "Dear [name]".
