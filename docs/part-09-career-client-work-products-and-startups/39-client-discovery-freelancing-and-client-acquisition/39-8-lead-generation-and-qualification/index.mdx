2. **[39.8.2 Building Targeted Prospect Lists](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/39-client-discovery-freelancing-and-client-acquisition/39-8-lead-generation-and-qualification/39-8-2-building-targeted-prospect-lists):** ICP first, sources and enrichment, hygiene rules, and the 50-name rule.

Define your ICP before touching a tool. A list without ICP is just email addresses.
