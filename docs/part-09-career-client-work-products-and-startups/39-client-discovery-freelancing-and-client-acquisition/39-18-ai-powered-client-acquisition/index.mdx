AI changes client acquisition by making research and follow-up faster — not by replacing judgment, relationships, or the word "thank you". This subchapter shows you how to use LLMs and enrichment tools as force multipliers while keeping your voice human.

2. **[39.18.2 Automating Follow-Ups Without Losing Authenticity](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/39-client-discovery-freelancing-and-client-acquisition/39-18-ai-powered-client-acquisition/39-18-2-automating-follow-ups-without-losing-authenticity):** Human-in-the-loop follow-ups, 3-touch sequence, what never to automate.

## How to Use This Subchapter

Don't outsource relationships. Use AI to compress grunt work; you supply the judgment.
