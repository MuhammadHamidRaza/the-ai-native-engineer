Follow-up is where most AI-driven outbound dies. This lesson teaches you to automate the repetitive parts — timing, templates, logging — while keeping every send looking (and feeling) like you wrote it with care.

## 1. What to Automate (and What Never To)

| Automate | Never automate |
| :-- | :-- |
| Scheduling follow-ups (Day 3/7/14) | The hook itself |
| Logging opens/replies in a tracker | The decision to stop |
| Template scaffolding with placeholders | The sentence that names their pain |
| Unsubscribe logging | The final "worth it?" judgment |

## 2. The Human-in-the-Loop Follow-Up Loop

1. AI drafts follow-up from conversation context.
2. You review in ≤2 min; tweak one sentence to sound like you.
3. Send; log outcome.

This preserves speed without the robot voice that prospects filter instantly.

## 3. The 3-Touch AI Sequence

Day 0 — initial with hook. Day 4 — "Thoughts on X?" with one added insight. Day 10 — final polite status request with an easy out. No fourth touch; it's noise.

## 4. Common Failure Modes

- **Full automation** — zero personal touch; open rates crater.
- **Generic AI openers** — "I hope this email finds you well."
- **Ignoring replies** — bots can't handle nuanced objections; route to you.

## 5. Exercise

Design your AI-assisted follow-up loop in 5 bullet steps. Run it on 3 real prospects; note reply rate vs generic outreach.

**Acceptance criteria.** Your loop includes a human review gate, a 3-touch template, and unsubscribe logging; replies are handled by you, not the bot.
