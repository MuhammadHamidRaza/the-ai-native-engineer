AI doesn't replace judgment — it compresses the grunt work. This lesson teaches you to use LLMs and enrichment tools to research prospects in minutes instead of hours, while keeping the human signal that actually closes deals.

## 1. The AI Research Stack

- **Company brief generator** — paste URL → LLM produces: product, stack guess, recent milestones, likely pains, fit score.
- **Job postings analysis** — "hiring platform engineer" + their blog = your hook in one shot.
- **RFP/Tender parsing** — extract deadlines, budget, evaluation criteria instantly.
- **News/press monitoring** — funding, acquisitions, incidents — your trigger events.

## 2. The 10-Minute Prospect Brief

```
PROMPT (paste into any LLM):
"Given this URL and job posting, output:
1. Product + ICP (2 sentences)
2. Public pain signals (incidents, scaling posts, hiring)
3. One likely technical bottleneck
4. One business risk if unfixed
5. Best opening line for outreach
Company: ____
Job URL: ____"
```

Then verify every claim — AI hallucinates plausibly; check their blog and StackShare.

## 3. Enrichment Tools

Apollo/Clay for emails; Wappalyzer for stack; BuiltWith for tech mix; Hunter.io for verification. Never send unverified addresses.

## 4. Common Failure Modes

- **Trusting AI output blindly** — one wrong technical claim destroys credibility instantly.
- **Over-automating** — mass-personalized "Hi , saw you're from".
- **Ignoring human hook** — the best brief is still built on something you saw with your own eyes.

## 5. Exercise

Run 3 prospect URLs through an AI brief generator. Verify every claim against the live site/blog; score accuracy. Use the best brief to draft one outreach message.

**Acceptance criteria.** Your brief contains ≥3 verified claims, one likely pain with evidence, and one honest opening line.
