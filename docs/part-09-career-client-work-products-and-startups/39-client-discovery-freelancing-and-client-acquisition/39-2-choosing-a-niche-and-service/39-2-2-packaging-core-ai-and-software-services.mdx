A service is not a list of things you can do — it's a **named, bounded, priced outcome** a buyer can say yes to in one reading. Unwrapped skills read as risk; wrapped packages read as professionalism. This lesson turns your engineering capabilities into three sellable packages with clear scope walls.

## 1. The Packaging Formula

`Package = Target pain + Guarantee boundary + Timeframe + Price band + Deliverables`

Worked example:

```text
"AI Support Deflection Audit — 2 weeks, $4,500"

You send me: 500 recent support tickets.
You get:  (1) ticket-category report, (2) top-5 deflection opportunities with est. deflection rate,
          (3) architecture sketch (RAG over your docs/FAQ), (4) 12-week implementation roadmap,
          (5) go/no-go recommendation.
No obligation. If we proceed, the audit fee is credited.
```

Every element does work: **500 tickets** makes it bounded, **2 weeks** bounds time, **$4,500** bounds price, **deliverables list** bounds scope, **credit offer** converts the audit into the engagement naturally.

## 2. Build Your Three Packages

Most engineering consultants run three:

| Tier | Purpose | Example (AI/software) |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Offer 1 — Foot in the door** | Low-commitment, fast, proves value | "Checkout Performance Audit, $1,500, 4 days" |
| **Offer 2 — Flagship project** | The core business, repeatable | "HelpBot Deflection Build, $18k, 6 weeks" |
| **Offer 3 — Retainer/continuity** | Ongoing value, stable cash | "Fractional AI/Platform Engineer, $4k/mo, 20 hrs" |

**Offer 1 is the Trojan horse** — 60–80% of audit clients convert to Offer 2 within 90 days when the audit is genuinely good.

## 3. Scope Walls — The Deliverable Menu

For each package, enumerate exactly what's included and explicitly what isn't:

```text
Offer 2 (HelpBot Deflection Build):
INCLUDED: RAG pipeline (pgvector), 30-policy doc ingestion, hybrid search,
citation checking, refusal guardrails, admin review UI, 25-case eval suite,
deployment to your Render/Railway, 2 training sessions, 30-day bugfix window.
NOT INCLUDED: hosting costs, ongoing model API costs, new policy authoring,
non-English content, CRM/Zendesk write actions, on-call rotation.
```

An explicit NOT list is the cheapest insurance you own. Clients respect it; scope-creep respects it more.

## 4. Pricing Anchor Construction

- **Research the alternatives**: what would an agency charge? What would a full-time hire cost? Your package is the sane middle — show the math.
- **Two visible tiers** beat three: "Standard $12k / Premium $18k (includes eval suite + 90 days tweaks)" — anchors the middle, and Premium sells more often than you'd guess.
- **Never** quote hourly as the default for a defined package. It hides your efficiency and punishes you for getting faster.

## 5. Test the Package Before You Announce It

Post it in a niche community (or 1:1 with 5 prospects): "I'm piloting a $4,500 AI Support Audit for Shopify merchants — would that help?" Their objections are your to-do list: unclear deliverables, suspicious price, missing proof. Fix, then launch.

## 6. The One-Page Sales Sheet

Every package gets a single page with: pain in their words → proof (case study or number) → offer → deliverables → timeline → price → FAQ → CTA ("book the 15-minute audit call" + calendar link). This page does 80% of your closing when your outreach lands.

## 7. Exercise

Productize one package for your chosen niche: name, pain, deliverables (incl. 3 explicit exclusions), timeline, two-tier price, one-page sales sheet draft. Test it on 3 people in the niche and record their verbatim objections; iterate.

**Acceptance criteria.** Your package has fixed price, fixed timeline, ≥4 deliverables, ≥3 explicit exclusions, a two-tier anchor, and zero unrequested line items.
