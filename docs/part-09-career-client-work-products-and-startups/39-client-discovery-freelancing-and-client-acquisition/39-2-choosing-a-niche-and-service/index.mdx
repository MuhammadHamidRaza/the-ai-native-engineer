Specialists get hired; generalists get bid on. This subchapter teaches you to pick a niche with actual buyer money behind it — using five measurable filters — and to package your capability into three offers a buyer can say yes to in one reading.

## Lessons in Subchapter 39.2

1. **[39.2.1 Evaluating High-Value Technical Niches](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/39-client-discovery-freelancing-and-client-acquisition/39-2-choosing-a-niche-and-service/39-2-1-evaluating-high-value-technical-niches):** The five-filter test, the three niche archetypes, a one-weekend validation protocol, and your 6-word positioning statement.
2. **[39.2.2 Packaging Core AI & Software Services](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/39-client-discovery-freelancing-and-client-acquisition/39-2-choosing-a-niche-and-service/39-2-2-packaging-core-ai-and-software-services):** The audit → flagship → retainer product ladder, explicit scope walls, two-tier anchor pricing, and the one-page sales sheet.

## How to Use This Subchapter

Don't skip the validation weekend in 39.2.1 — every consultant who picks a niche from a couch ends up selling to people who don't buy. The door-open test (5 messages, 2 replies) is your go/no-go signal.
