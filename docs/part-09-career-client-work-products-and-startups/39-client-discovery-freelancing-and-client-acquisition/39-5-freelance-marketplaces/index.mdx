## The Shared Marketplace Playbook

1. **Profile = first interview** — niche title, 3 strong portfolio items, 90%+ response rate target.
2. **Bid selectively** — fewer, better proposals (5/week) beat 50 spray bids.
3. **Escrow/protection first** — never work unfunded; milestone or pre-funded account only.
4. **Over-deliver on review week** — the review is the compounding asset.
5. **Graduate** — after 2–3 strong reviews, move steady clients off-platform (where allowed) for direct relationships.

## Graduation Signals

You're ready to graduate when: you reject >80% of invitations for being low-quality, one platform gives you consistent flow, or clients ask to hire you directly. Keep marketplaces as a funnel, not a crutch.
