PeoplePerHour (PPH) is a UK-born marketplace solving proximity-and-trust: British and European SMBs want English-first engineers in their timezone, who understand UK business norms and invoice like professionals — without the offshore lottery. For UK-based engineers PPH is a **geographic moat to defend**; the smaller pond is the point.

## 1. What PeoplePerHour Is Good For in 2026 (and for Whom)

Good for: UK/EU SMB builds and rescues, local-business integrations (booking systems, Stripe/GoCardless, Xero), WordPress/Shopify e-commerce glue, ongoing retainers with agencies white-labeling you, and regulated-ish buyers who distrust anonymous global platforms. Whoever writes idiomatic British English in proposals, answers calls in UK hours, and bills through UK banking/VAT flows wins trust disproportionately. Weak for: offshoring the language gap, or expecting to compete globally against 20%-below-market bids — PPH buyers often specifically pay for UK presence.

## 2. Who Buys There and at What Budget

Practitioner ranges:

| Buyer | Typical deal | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| UK micro-SMBs | £100–£1,500 | Fixes, migrations, integrations |
| UK agencies | £500–£10,000 per sprint | Recurring subcontracting — best tier |
| e-commerce owners | £200–£5,000 | Shopify/WooCommerce work |
| EU SMBs (DACH/EU timezone-aligned) | £200–£6,000 | Overlap advantage vs US platforms |
| Retainers | £500–£3,000/month | Support + minor builds |

Budgets are smaller than Toptal and respectable vs US platforms; the UK timezone advantage plus fast same-day replies routinely closes deals a cheaper distant bidder cannot.

## 3. Profile and Positioning That Wins

- **English and locale signals:** UK spelling, GBP pricing, VAT-registered status if applicable, UK phone or video presence — these materially lift shortlist rates with UK buyers.
- **UK-typed portfolio artifacts:** screenshots with GBP figures, UK client contexts, mention of GDPR/ICO-aware practices where relevant.
- **Accreditation:** PPH Certification/accreditation badges for specific skills (code-reviewed profiles for approved categories) are a major ranking and trust lever — apply once your profile has real delivered work.
- **Rates:** mid-to-premium vs the marketplace floor; PPH buyers discount price chasing less than other platforms, but you will not charge Toptal rates here.

Worked example (profile headline + intro line):

```text
"UK-based Next.js/Postgres engineer — 300+ Shopify & bespoke builds,
GDPR-aware, day-rate £350. Fixed-scope audits £450, migrations from £1,200.
Same-day replies UK time; insured and VAT-registered."
```

## 4. How the Platform's System Works: Hourlies, Credits, Accreditation

- **Hourlies:** PPH's fixed-scope, packaged offers (gig-like). Publish 3–6 hourlies covering your audits and sprints; they sell while you bid.
- **Bidding:** buyers post jobs; freelancers get a limited number of free proposals per month; extra proposals cost credits. Buy credits sparingly — spray bidding burns money.
- **£-tier fee model:** service fee per **buyer lifetime billing** — roughly 20% under ~£250, 7.5% to ~£5,000, 3.5% above (PPH also introduced a monthly scheme around 20% on the first ~£500 then ~5%; older accounts may sit under different terms — check your plan). The per-buyer tiers reward stickiness: keep one UK buyer for 18 months and your fee collapses to 3.5%.
- **Ranking:** certification, review count/quality, response rate, on-time delivery, profile completeness. UK clients browse category pages — being near the top there is worth more than winning individual auctions.

## 5. The Repeatable Workflow

1. **Find:** filter jobs to UK/EU buyers, posted ≤7 days, UK-budget signals, verified payment; check buyer score and spend.
2. **Qualify:** scope clarity, timeline vs your UK hours, are they credit-budgeting or outcome-buying (pay better)?
3. **Propose:** UK-English, reference one past UK-style client (anonymized), name a GBP fixed price or hourly rate, ask 1–2 scoping questions; keep under 180 words.
4. **Close:** 15-min call in UK hours; written mini-spec + milestone split (often 50/50).
5. **Deliver:** UK-hours responsiveness; short weekly written update; invoice through PPH only.
6. **Review:** ask for the review on delivery; repeat buyers drop you into the 7.5%/3.5% tiers.

## 6. Fees and Payment Protection

Fees per buyer-tier (or monthly scheme — verify): effective 7.5–3.5% on mature UK relationships is the competitive edge. Client pays a separate buyer-side fee (percent + sometimes minimum) — worth noting in negotiation. Protection: **Escrow (deposit) on award** — buyer deposits before work; PPH releases on milestone sign-off; disputes go through their dispute flow with workroom evidence. Do not ship before the deposit lands. VAT handling is cleaner via PPH invoicing than off-platform UK deals; PPH invoices are valid UK records.

## 7. Limits, Scams, Platform Rules

Smaller pool — feast/famine across months; UK SMB budgets cap your ceiling. Scams mirror other marketplaces: requests to continue on email pre-award (ToS violation — circumvention risk), "test jobs" designed to harvest work, phishing brief links, and fake "verified buyer" lookalikes checking your bank details. Rules: no off-platform contact pre-award without buyer-initiated escalation, no duplicate accounts, no review swaps; off-platform circumvention is a ban offense. GDPR cuts both ways — never upload client production data into your own demo environments.

## 8. KPIs and a Realistic Ramp Timeline

| KPI | Practitioner benchmark |
| :-- | :-- |
| Proposals sent / month | 8–15 targeted (credits limit spraying) |
| Reply/shortlist rate | 20–30% with UK positioning |
| Win rate | 15–30% of shortlists |
| Buyer retention | Track per-buyer fee tier drift |
| Time to first £ | 2–6 weeks (practitioner range) |

Expect slower inbound but higher close rates than global auction sites; month 3 usually shows first repeat reviews.

## 9. Graduation Signals — When to Leave

Graduate when UK clients start referring you directly (real graduation signal on PPH), your pipeline of direct UK retainers exceeds 50% of revenue, or you consistently lose to cheaper global bids (means you're competing on mass-market briefs, not UK trust). Keep PPH as inbound for UK SMBs even later — the fee collapse on long-term buyers makes it cheap acquisition.

## 10. Exercise

1. Verify which fee scheme your account is on; model your effective rate at £2k / £6k per-buyer lifetime billing.
2. Publish 2 hourlies plus 10 UK-filtered proposals; log response rates.
3. Apply for the relevant PPH certification; draft the application artifact now.

**Acceptance criteria.** Documented fee math at three buyer-spend levels; two live hourlies; 10 logged targeted proposals with UK-English quality pass; certification application or written plan with evidence requirements.
