Guru is the quiet veteran: a bid-and-quote model with an escrow spine (SafePay) and a union-hall feel — older, US-Midwest-heavy client base, far less auction chaos than Freelancer.com, and fees you can plan around. It solves: **credible, escrow-protected deals without Upwork's competition density or Fiverr's 20% tax.** The trade: fewer jobs posted daily; rolodex pace, not firehose.

## 1. What Guru Is Good For in 2026 (and for Whom)

Good for: mid-range US/Canada business work — legacy-system rescues, Access/SQL fixes, small API integrations, e-commerce back-offices, NetSuite/QuickBooks glue, desktop tooling, ongoing maintenance retainers for agencies. Practitioners who price calmly, submit disciplined quotes, and leverage SafePay win here. Weak for: bleeding-edge AI product work, EU/UK-first buyers (PPH wins), or anyone wanting 30 fresh briefs daily (Upwork/Fiverr win). Guru is a slow-burn, high-trust channel — treat quotes like lightweight proposals, not volume bids.

## 2. Who Buys There and at What Budget

Practitioner ranges:

| Buyer | Typical project | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| US SMBs (established, 10–100 staff) | $300–$5,000 | Legacy fixes, integrations |
| US agencies | $1,000–$20,000 / sprint | Subcontract overflow — best lane |
| Mid-market ops teams | $2,000–$30,000 | Internal tools, data cleanup |
| Nonprofits / education | $200–$4,000 | Tight budgets, high loyalty |
| Retainers | $500–$4,000/month | On-call support |

Expect smaller deal sizes than Toptal and fewer applicants per job than the global sites — margins come from less competition, not higher rates.

## 3. Profile and Positioning That Wins

- **Quote-first credibility:** Guru buyers read the quote before the profile; your quote must demonstrate you understood the brief (echo their problem, itemize scope, name milestones).
- **Verification stack:** phone + ID verification, a complete portfolio with quantified outcomes, and certifications where relevant — Guru surfaces these prominently.
- **Membership tier interplay:** Basic is free with a limited quote allowance per month (~10); paid memberships lift the allowance and cut your job fee (below).
- **Niche declaration:** "Legacy .NET + SQL Server rescues for US distributors" outranks "available for hire" — Guru's category browse is how most buyers find you.

Worked example (quote opening):

```text
"Your QuickBooks→Shopify reconciliation script breaks on multi-tax orders —
classic line-tax rounding drift. I fixed this exact flow for a distributor
last quarter: 1,900 orders/mo, zero write-offs for 6 months.
Plan: audit (day 1), patch + backfill (days 2–3), runbook (day 4). $1,400 fixed,
SafePay milestones 50/50. Available Thursday US Eastern hours."
```

## 4. How the System Actually Works: Quotes, Membership Tiers, SafePay

- **Quotes, not bids:** you send a Quote (scope + price) per job; employers shortlist and award. No per-quote fee on Basic beyond the monthly allowance cap; overages require paid tier.
- **Job fees by membership:** roughly **9% (Basic free) → 7% → 6% → 5%** as membership rises; employers may contribute up to ~5% toward your job fee on some jobs. Confirm current tiers — Guru revises them.
- **SafePay (the centerpiece):** employer funds the milestone into SafePay *before* work starts; you see the funded amount; on sign-off, funds release to you. No SafePay = you are working unsecured — walk away or add short milestones.
- **Visibility:** profile completeness, verification badges, quote response rate, reviews, and membership level all feed ranking on category pages — no JSS, no Connects, no boost auctions.

## 5. The Repeatable Workflow

1. **Find:** filter to payment-verified employers; check "SafePay payment option" flag; ≤10 quotes already submitted; posted this week; real budget band.
2. **Qualify:** brief names a system, an owner, and a date; avoid "$100 quick job" hagglers fishing for free scoping.
3. **Quote:** mirror problem in line 1; itemize 2–4 milestones; state SafePay 50/50; ask 1–2 questions; include one sentence on edge cases you already see.
4. **Close:** clarify on a call if offered; never start before SafePay funding is visible.
5. **Deliver:** milestone sign-offs with written summaries; workroom logs everything for disputes.
6. **Review:** request review at final release; repeat employers are the whole economics.

## 6. Fees and Payment Protection

Effective take: 9% Basic / as low as ~5% paid tiers, minus any employer contribution. On retained UK/US buyers over time, 5–6% is materially cheaper than Fiverr's 20% or Freelancer.com's 10%-with-minimums. Withdrawal fees vary by method (check current). Payment protection is the model's strength: SafePay arbitration handles disputes with workroom evidence; without funding, **no protection exists** — Guru cannot rescue verbal deals.

## 7. Limits, Scams, Platform Rules

Smaller demand: slow weeks happen; do not mortgage rent against Guru-only pipeline. Scams: "start before SafePay funded" coercion, fake employer verifications, requests to continue off-Guru pre-award (ToS violation and your only fraud vector), overly eager partial-payment demands, and refund-baiting after delivery. Rules: account sharing banned; off-platform circumvention sanctioned; fake milestones to launder reviews trigger account review; misquote bait (win with $1 then renegotiate) kills your standing.

## 8. KPIs and a Realistic Ramp Timeline

| KPI | Practitioner benchmark |
| :-- | :-- |
| Quotes per month | 10–20 targeted (below allowance waste) |
| Shortlist/reply rate | 20–35% |
| Win rate | 15–30% of shortlists |
| Quote → funded SafePay | Track; decline funding-delay jobs |
| Time to first paid deal | 2–8 weeks |

Practitioner timeline: week 1–2 profile + verification + 3 case studies; weeks 2–4 first quotes; month 2 first SafePay award is common for verified specialists.

## 9. Graduation Signals — When to Leave

Graduate when your inbound from repeat US SMBs becomes predictable enough to go retainer-only; when you win quotes primarily on speed-of-reply rather than competence; or when direct US client pipeline via referrals exceeds ~40% of revenue. Guru remains a solid overflow and escrow tool even after graduation — SafePay is a useful instrument for cautious new direct clients.
