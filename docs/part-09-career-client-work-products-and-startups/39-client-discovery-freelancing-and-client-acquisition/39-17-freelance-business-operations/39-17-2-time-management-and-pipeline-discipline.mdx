Freelancing without pipeline discipline is a boom-bust cycle that burns out the best engineers. This lesson teaches you to run a lightweight weekly system that keeps utilization steady and stress low.

## 1. The Weekly Freelance Cadence

- **Mon** — pipeline review: active leads, where each sits, next action.
- **Tue–Thu** — deep work on active engagements.
- **Fri** — admin: invoices, follow-ups, proposal updates, next week's pipeline.

## 2. The Pipeline Dashboard

Columns: Lead → Qualified → Proposal sent → Negotiation → Closed → Delivered → Retained. One row per deal, updated weekly. 3 deals minimum in Qualified at all times.

## 3. Time-Blocking for Freelancers

Guard 2 deep-work blocks (4h each) for delivery; 1 shallow block for comms/admin; 1 sales block for outreach. Track billable vs non-billable hours — aim >60% billable.

## 4. Common Failure Modes

- **No pipeline review** — feast-famine cycles; dead months surprise you.
- **All delivery, zero sales** — pipeline dries up mid-project.
- **No admin rhythm** — invoices forgotten, taxes underestimated, burnout.
- **Working unpaid on proposals** — cap proposal time; a proposal >4h is a discovery-call failure in disguise.
- **No lead floor** — floor of 3 qualified leads at all times; below it, outreach is your top priority regardless of delivery pressure.

## 5. Worked Pipeline Example (Real Shape, Fictional Names)

```text
Fri Jan 10 — weekly review (20 min, standing)

LEAD                    STAGE          NEXT ACTION            DUE
Brightside Dental       Proposal sent  Recap email + Q       Jan 13
Cascade Fitness        Qualified      Send 1-page scope     Jan 14
Nori Sushi (referral)  Negotiation    3pm call Tue          Jan 14
Harbor Physical Therapy Qualified      Share Loom demo       Jan 15
Fern Valley Vets       Discovery done Send proposal          Jan 16
Oak Street Cafe        Lost (budget)  Revisit in 90 days    Apr 10

Qualified count: 2 (below floor of 3) → Monday: 10 new outbound emails
Proposal outstanding: 1 ($8,000 — awaiting signature since Jan 6)
Utilization last week: 34h billable / 42h total = 81%
Invoices outstanding: INV-0023 $4,000 Net-14 (Jan 2) → day-8 reminder today
```

**Read this dashboard:** two qualified leads (needs top-up), one unsigned proposal (flag the follow-up), one overdue invoice (act today), one 90-day revisit queued. Ten things you'd miss in your head — one screen, 20 minutes.

## 6. Exercise

Build your one-page pipeline dashboard in a spreadsheet (columns: Lead / Stage / Next action / Due / Amount / Source). Fill in your real pipeline (or the CareQueue scenario if starting fresh): include at least one Qualified, one Proposal sent, one Lost-with-revisit, one Delivered. Set a recurring **Fri 10:00 "pipeline review"** 20-minute calendar block.

**Acceptance criteria.** Dashboard shows current stage for ≥3 leads with dated next actions and amounts; your calendar holds a 20-minute Friday pipeline block plus 2x4h protected deep-work blocks and 1 sales block; utilization ≥60% is the target. You can state your floor (3 Qualified) and what you'll do tomorrow if you're below it.
