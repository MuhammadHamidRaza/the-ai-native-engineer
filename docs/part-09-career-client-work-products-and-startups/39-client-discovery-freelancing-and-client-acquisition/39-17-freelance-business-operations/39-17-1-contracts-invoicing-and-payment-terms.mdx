Nothing destroys a freelance business faster than unclear agreements and late payments. This lesson teaches you the paperwork stack that makes you look like a serious business and gets you paid on time.

## 1. The Contract Starter Kit

- **One-page services agreement** — scope, deliverables, acceptance, price, terms, IP.
- **Independent contractor clause** — your status; not employee; no benefits.
- **Payment terms** — deposit (50%), milestones, net-14/30, late fee (1.5%/mo).
- **IP transfer** — upon final payment only.
- **Confidentiality** — standard NDA language.

Use a template lawyer-reviewed once; reuse forever.

## 2. Invoicing That Gets Paid

- **Send same-day** after acceptance milestone.
- **Clear line items** — "HelpBot eval suite — 25 cases — $3,000" beats "development work — $3,000".
- **Payment links** — Stripe Invoicing, Wise, PayPal Business; multiple options.
- **Follow-up cadence** — day 3 after due date, one friendly reminder; day 10, firm; day 21, pause work clause.

## 3. Common Failure Modes

- **No written contract** — "we trust you" = you'll chase invoices forever.
- **Net-45 terms** — cash-flow suicide; default net-14.
- **No deposit** — you finance their business.
- **"Friendly" terms for the first client** — first clients are exactly when you need the terms most.
- **Verbal scope changes** — every change is a new line item; verbal never counts.

## 4. A Complete One-Page Services Agreement (Fill-In, Not Legal Advice)

This is a starting skeleton reviewed by lawyers as a class — have your own counsel review before first use; local law varies (contractor classification, IP assignment, liability caps).

```text
SERVICES AGREEMENT — [Your name/entity] ("Consultant") and [Client] ("Client"),
dated 2026-12-01.

1. SCOPE. Consultant will: (a) triage Brightside Dental frontdesk inbox via Gmail
   API (read-only), (b) deploy CareQueue pilot, (c) train Marisol for 2 sessions.
2. DELIVERABLES & ACCEPTANCE. M1 (wk 2): 4 labels + drafts live — demo + 14 days
   to accept/reject in writing; silence after 14 days = accepted.
   M3 (wk 5): 30-day review + handoff pack.
3. FEES & PAYMENT. $8,000 fixed. 50% ($4,000) due on signature; $2,000 at M1;
   $2,000 at M3. Net-14. Late fee 1.5%/mo after 14 days. Payment via
   Stripe/Wise; Client pays own transaction fees.
4. IP. All deliverables (code, docs, configs) transfer to Client **upon final
   payment only**. Consultant retains pre-existing IP (CareQueue platform)
   and grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use it.
5. CONFIDENTIALITY. Both parties keep the other's business/technical info
   confidential for 24 months. Client retains patient data — never exported.
6. TERM & TERMINATION. Either party may terminate with 14 days' written notice.
   Client pays for work completed through termination date.
7. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR. Consultant is not an employee; no taxes withheld;
   no benefits; Client controls outcomes, not methods.
8. LIABILITY. Cap: fees paid under this agreement. No indirect damages.
9. GOVERNING LAW. [Your jurisdiction]. Disputes: mediation before litigation.
```

**Clause-by-clause you can reuse:** 1-sentence scope + acceptance with a 14-day silence rule + fee table + IP-on-final-payment + termination-with-notice. If you never change these six, your paperwork survives 90% of engagements.

## 5. Exercise

Draft your one-page agreement using the § 4 skeleton for a mock $8,000 project. Include: deposit ≥50%, net-14, milestone acceptance with silence rule, IP-on-final-payment, independent-contractor clause, governing law of your jurisdiction.

**Acceptance criteria.** Your agreement: one page, names both parties/dated, ≥8 numbered clauses, deposit ≥50%, net-14 (or better), acceptance silence rule, IP-on-final-payment, governing law — ready to hand to your lawyer for a single review.
