2. **[39.17.2 Time Management and Pipeline Discipline](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/39-client-discovery-freelancing-and-client-acquisition/39-17-freelance-business-operations/39-17-2-time-management-and-pipeline-discipline):** Weekly cadence, pipeline dashboard, time-blocking.
