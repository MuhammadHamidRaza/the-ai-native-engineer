Chapter 38 is the engineering career operating manual — from resume to signed offer. Companies hire proofs, not intentions: this chapter teaches you to manufacture the proofs, manufacture the introductions, perform in the loop, and negotiate the outcome like a professional.

**Chapter 38 (`8 Focused Hours`)** covers:

- **38.1 Positioning, Proof & Applications** — bullet-engineered resumes, the GitHub audit recruiters actually perform, portfolios that finish the scroll, and a targeted outbound + referral system with a 60-touch funnel.
- **38.2 Interviews & Career Progression** — pattern-list coding with narration, the system-design template, STAR stories with numbers, AI-era loop realities, and offer negotiation: scorecards, anchoring, leveling, red flags, and the decade game.

## What Changes After This Chapter

| Before | After |
| :-- | :-- |
| Applies cold, hopes | 30-touch targeted funnel, warm introductions |
| Generic 3-page resume | One page, every bullet quantified |
| Silent whiteboard solving | Narrated, timed, interviewer-engaged |
| "What do they pay?" | Five-axis offer scorecard + negotiation scripts |
| Offer = validation | Offer = one move in a multi-year compounding game |

## Exit Criteria — You Are Done When You Can

- [ ] Deliver a one-page resume where every bullet carries a number
- [ ] Run your outbound funnel (10 targets, 5 personalized touches, follow-up logged)
- [ ] Solve medium pattern problems narrated out loud in 25 minutes
- [ ] Design "checkout with idempotency" end-to-end on a whiteboard in 35 minutes
- [ ] Tell six STAR stories with quantified results
- [ ] Score an offer on five axes and name your anchor + the non-comp items you'd trade
