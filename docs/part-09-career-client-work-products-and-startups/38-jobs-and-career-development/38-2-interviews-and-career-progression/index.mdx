Interviews are a skill, not a talent screen — and offers are negotiated like any other business transaction. This section prepares you to perform in the loop (coding under pressure, system design with judgment, behavioral answers with receipts) and to evaluate what comes back the way a professional does: money, scope, learning, trajectory, optionality.

## Lessons in Subchapter 38.2

1. **[38.2.1 Coding, System Design and Behavioral Interviews](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/38-jobs-and-career-development/38-2-interviews-and-career-progression/38-2-1-coding-system-design-and-behavioral-interviews):** Pattern-list coding with narration, the requirements → design → deep-dive template, STAR stories with numbers, AI-era loop adjustments, and mock drills.
2. **[38.2.2 Evaluating Offers and Long-Term Leverage](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/38-jobs-and-career-development/38-2-interviews-and-career-progression/38-2-2-evaluating-offers-and-long-term-leverage):** Total comp math, the five-axis scorecard, negotiation scripts and leverage, leveling as the hidden 25–40%, red flags, and the decade game.

## How to Use This Subchapter

Record yourself. Watched back, every flaw you know you have becomes visible. Ten days of deliberate mock interviews beats three months of passive grinding — schedule the first one this week.
