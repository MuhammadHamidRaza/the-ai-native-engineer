Before you interview, you must be findable — and worth calling. This section builds the two artifacts that get you into the room: an engineering resume that survives both ATS parsers and 15-second human scans, and a targeted outbound + referral system that produces conversations without spray-and-pray.

## Lessons in Subchapter 38.1

1. **[38.1.1 Resumes, GitHub Profiles and Engineering Portfolios](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/38-jobs-and-career-development/38-1-positioning-proof-and-applications/38-1-1-resumes-github-profiles-and-engineering-portfolios):** The bullet formula engineers trust, the 90-second GitHub audit, the five-section portfolio, track positioning, ATS reality in 2026.
2. **[38.1.2 Targeted Outbound and Referrals](/book/part-09-career-client-work-products-and-startups/38-jobs-and-career-development/38-1-positioning-proof-and-applications/38-1-2-targeted-outbound-and-referrals):** The referral math, target-list construction, personalized outreach, the ask that gets a yes, and pipeline discipline — 60 touches beats 500 sprays.

## How to Use This Subchapter

Build the artifacts before you need them — a good resume and a 10-target outbound list take one focused weekend. The people who ask "how do I get my first interview" after applying to 100 jobs skipped these two steps.
