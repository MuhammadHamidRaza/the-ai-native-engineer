Chapter 40 teaches the only product skill that matters at the early stage: thinking in outcomes, not features, and validating demand before writing code.

**Chapter 40 (`8 Focused Hours`)** covers:

- **40.1 Problem Validation & Product Scope** — Validating demand before code; defining the MVP.
- **40.2 Shipping & Iterating With Users** — Onboarding, analytics and activation; pricing and feedback cycles.

## Exit Criteria — You Are Done When You Can

- [ ] Run 10 customer interviews and score validation signals honestly
- [ ] Define a one-job MVP in ≤2 weeks with one activation metric
- [ ] Instrument onboarding and measure drop-off
- [ ] Choose and test a pricing model with real users
