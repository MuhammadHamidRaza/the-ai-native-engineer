Chapter 41 is where the product meets the market. You'll learn to launch with a clear positioning, acquire early adopters, measure unit economics honestly, and know when to grow the team.

**Chapter 41 (`8 Focused Hours`)** covers:

- **41.1 Go-To-Market & Launch Execution** — positioning, channels, early adopters.
- **41.2 Unit Economics & Sustainable Growth** — CAC/LTV, churn, inference margins, and hiring.

## Exit Criteria — You Are Done When You Can

- [ ] Write one-sentence positioning for your product
- [ ] Pick and execute one distribution channel for 30 days
- [ ] Estimate CAC, LTV, and inference margin
- [ ] Know when to hire your first team member
