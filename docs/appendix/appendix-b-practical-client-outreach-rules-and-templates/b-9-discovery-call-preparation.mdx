**What this solves:** You have the call in 48 hours and no idea what to ask. Most discovery calls drift into friendly chat and close nothing. This checklist makes every call a pipeline step, not a social event.

## 60-Minute Pre-Call Checklist

```text
[ ] Company: recent news, funding, job posts, tech stack (Wappalyzer)
[ ] Person: their role, how long, what they own, what they're measured on
[ ] Problem: your one-sentence hypothesis of their pain
[ ] Agenda: 4 items, 45 minutes, sent to them in advance
[ ] Materials: screen share ready — their site, your 1-slide architecture
[ ] Goal: book the proposal call or leave with a defined next step
```

## The Call Agenda (Sent 24h Before)

```text
Hi Alice — here's the agenda for tomorrow's 45 minutes:

0:00–0:05  Warm open
0:05–0:15  Your world: team, stack, current process
0:15–0:25  The pain: what's broken, what it costs
0:25–0:35  What good looks like (their words for success)
0:35–0:45  Next steps: proposal vs. deeper audit

Bring webhook logs if handy — but not required.
```

## The 10 Questions That Unlock Budget

1. "What does your team look like today?"
2. "How are you handling [thing] right now?"
3. "What broke most recently — walk me through it?"
4. "What does that cost you per month, roughly?"
5. "Who else feels this internally?"
6. "What have you tried?"
7. "If this stayed broken for another year, what happens?"
8. "What's your timeline?"
9. "Is budget approved, or exploratory?"
10. "If we fixed this in 4 weeks, what would change for you personally?"

## During the Call — Listening Discipline

- **80/20 rule** — they talk 80%, you talk 20%
- **Never pitch in the first 15 minutes**
- **Repeat their pain back in your words** before proposing anything
- **Take notes visibly** — they feel heard
- **Ask "why" twice** — root cause beats symptom every time

## After the Call — Within 2 Hours

```text
"Alice — great talking today. Recap:
 — Problem: [one sentence]
 — Impact: [$ number]
 — Timeline: [date]
I'll send the 1-page proposal by tomorrow EOD."
```

**Always send within 24 hours — speed is proof you listened.**
