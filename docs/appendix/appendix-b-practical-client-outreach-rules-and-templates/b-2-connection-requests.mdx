**What this solves:** A blank connection request gets ignored. A specific, low-pressure request gets accepted — and opens the door to conversation. This section gives you the exact wording.

## The Connection Request Templates

**Best — reference their content (highest accept rate):**

```text
Hi [Name] — saw your post on [topic] and found the [specific detail]
insight sharp. I work with [niche] on [outcome]. Would be good to
connect — no pitch, just following your work.
```

**Good — reference shared context:**

```text
Hi [Name] — we're both in [community/space]. Your [something specific]
caught my eye. Connecting to follow along — cheers.
```

**Acceptable — cold but specific:**

```text
Hi [Name] — I research [niche] and your [company] keeps coming up as a
leader in [specific area]. Connecting to learn — thanks.
```

**Never send:**

```text
"Hi, I'd like to add you to my professional network" — ignored
"Hi, I help companies with AI" — blocked
"Hi, let's connect" — generic; 20% accept rate instead of 50%+
```

## The Connection Request Rules

1. Always mention one specific thing — their post, their company, their talk
2. Never attach a pitch, calendar link, or attachment
3. Keep it under 300 characters
4. Send 10–15 personalized requests/week, not 100 generic ones
5. If they accept, wait for them to engage before messaging — or send one thank-you + one question
6. If they accept and go silent, comment on their posts for 2 weeks, then ask one question

## Follow-Up After Accept

```text
Thanks for connecting. I saw [specific post] — your point on [thing]
stuck with me. I'm exploring [problem area]; curious how you're thinking
about it?
```

**Not:** "Thanks for connecting! Are you free Tuesday for a call?" — never.

## Tracking Acceptances

| Name | Accepted? | Engaged? | Next step |
| :-- | :--: | :--: | :-- |
| Alice @ BrightCo | Y | Y | Comment on post, then ask question |
| Ben @ ShopDirect | Y | N | Engage on 2 posts, then message |

**Target:** Aim for a strong acceptance rate on well-targeted, personalized requests. Below ~30% suggests your profile or note needs work; above ~60% suggests you're clearly relevant to them. (Practitioner heuristics — LinkedIn does not publish these rates and they vary widely; measure your own.)
