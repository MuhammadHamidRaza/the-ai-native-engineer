**What this solves:** You need to send one proposal, one page, that closes — not a novel, not a PDF with stock photos. These templates are copy-paste ready; replace the brackets and send.

## Template 1: Full-Stack / AI Project (Project-Based)

```text
CLIENT: [Name] · PROJECT: [Title]

PROBLEM (their words):
"[Quote their exact pain back at them]"

OUTCOME:
[Specific metric + timeframe — e.g., "Checkout 401s from 12% → <1% in 4 weeks"]

SCOPE:
Week 1 — Audit + idempotency-key design (day 3 spec delivered)
Week 2–3 — Build: webhook layer, retry matrix, monitoring dashboard
Week 4 — Deploy + team training + 30-day support

INVESTMENT: $[X]
  50% upfront, 50% on go-live
  Includes deployment + 30-day bug-fix window

OUT OF SCOPE:
- New payment provider migration
- Backend rewrite beyond the checkout service
- Ongoing retainer (quoted separately if desired)

TIMELINE: Start [date] · Live [date]
```

## Template 2: Fractional / Retainer

```text
CLIENT: [Name] · ENGAGEMENT: Fractional AI Engineer · 20 hrs/mo

OUTCOME:
Own [domain] end-to-end: [specific mission — e.g., "support deflection for SaaS tier-1"]

MONTHLY DELIVERABLES:
- 2 production features or fixes per month (PR reviewed)
- Weekly 30-min async update every Friday
- Monthly metrics review: tickets deflected, latency, cost
- On-call for production incidents (WhatsApp, 4h response)

INVESTMENT: $[X]/month · Net 14 · 3-month minimum, then month-to-month

SCOPE BOUNDARY: beyond 20 hrs/month quoted at hourly rate ([hourly rate]/hr); changes need a 1-line written OK
```

## Template 3: Audit / Discovery Only

```text
CLIENT: [Name] · ENGAGEMENT: Technical Audit · 1 week · $[X]

OUTCOME:
Root-cause report on [problem] with a ranked fix list and ROI model.

DELIVERABLES:
- 1-page executive summary
- Full technical report (5–10 pages)
- 2 prioritized fixes with time estimates
- 45-min readout call

NO OBLIGATION TO BUILD — you own the report regardless
```

## The Proposal Rules

1. One page maximum
2. Their words first — never your capabilities
3. One measurable outcome
4. Explicit out-of-scope (prevents scope creep)
5. Simple payment terms (50/50 or Net 14)
6. Send within 48 hours of the demo

## The Follow-Up After Sending

```text
"Ben — proposal attached. The two timelines are: 4 weeks for the full
fix, or 1 week for the audit if you want to validate before committing.
Either works — which makes sense for your Q1 plan?"
```
