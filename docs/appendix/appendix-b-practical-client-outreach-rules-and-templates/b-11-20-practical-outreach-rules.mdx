**What this solves:** You understand the principles; now you need the checklist you run before every send. These 20 rules are the filter between "message sent" and "message replied."

## The 20 Rules

1. **Never send a message you wouldn't want to receive** — read it out loud first.
2. **One idea per message** — two ideas = two ignored ideas.
3. **Their world first, your world never** — open with something about them.
4. **Numbers beat adjectives** — "12% abandonment" beats "high".
5. **One question only** — multiple questions = no answer.
6. **No attachments in cold outreach** — links trigger spam filters and distrust.
7. **Under 100 words** — busy people skim; respect that.
8. **No "just checking in"** — every message must add new value or insight.
9. **Proof before promise** — show one artifact, not three promises.
10. **Ask for the smallest next step** — "one reply" beats "a call".
11. **Warm-up before pitch** — 2 weeks of engagement first, or you're noise.
12. **Personalize or don't send** — if you can't name one specific thing, don't hit send.
13. **Timing matters** — Tue–Thu 9–11am their time; never Friday 5pm.
14. **Follow up exactly twice** — then stop; noise is free, attention isn't.
15. **Use their name** — once, naturally; not three times (creepy).
16. **Write like a human** — contractions, short sentences, no jargon.
17. **Sign with your name + one line of proof** — "Hamid — checkout reliability for Shopify stores"
18. **Check your links work** — broken links = instant credibility loss.
19. **Track everything** — opens, replies, calls, proposals, closes — or you'll repeat mistakes.
20. **Review monthly** — what worked, what didn't; kill what failed; double what worked.

## The Pre-Send Checklist (Print This)

```text
[ ] Specific observation from their world (with a number if possible)
[ ] One question (open, low-cost to answer)
[ ] Zero attachments, zero links
[ ] 80-120 words
[ ] Sent at Tue–Thu 9–11am their time
[ ] I'd be happy to receive this message myself
[ ] I know what I'll do if they reply (vs. if they don't)
```

## The Review Loop

Every Friday, look at your numbers:

- Sent: X · Replied: Y · Calls: Z · Proposals: W · Closed: V
- Reply rate too low? → Your targeting or personalization is off
- Calls great, closes weak? → Your proposal or closing needs work

**The meta-rule:** outreach is a system, not a lottery. Run it weekly, measure honestly, iterate ruthlessly.
