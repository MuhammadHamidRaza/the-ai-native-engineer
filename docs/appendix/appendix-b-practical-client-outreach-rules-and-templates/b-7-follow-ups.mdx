**What this solves:** Deals rarely close on the first touch; most "not yet" replies come after a couple of follow-ups. Most engineers either don't follow up or follow up badly. This section gives you the cadence and language — practitioner heuristics, not a guarantee of anything.

## The Follow-Up Cadence

```text
Day 1 (after proposal):  "Did you get a chance to look at the proposal?"
Day 4:                   "Quick check-in — any questions so far?"
Day 9:                   "Last touch — happy to walk through it live; or totally no worries."
(Day 10 — stop)

```

**After a demo, not a proposal:**

```text
Day 1:  "Great talking today — sending the proposal tomorrow."
Day 4:  "Proposal attached; happy to tweak anything."
Day 9:  "Last touch, with one new insight — not a check-in."
(Then stop.)
```

## Follow-Up Messages That Don't Annoy

**Bad:** "Just checking in…"
**Good:** "Alice — noticed Shopify released webhook v3.1 yesterday; the change actually simplifies the idempotency fix I described. Want me to update the spec?"

**Bad:** "Bumping this…"
**Good:** "Ben — saw BrightCo just raised a Series A. Congrats — that means checkout uptime matters 10× more now. Want to lock in the slot before board season?"

**Bad:** "Are you still interested?"
**Good:** "Carol — one thing I forgot to mention: the fix works for your existing Stripe webhooks too, so the integration is near-zero friction for your team."

## The Final Follow-Up (Stop Signal)

```text
"Alice — I've followed up a few times now and don't want to be noise.
If the timing isn't right or the budget isn't approved, totally understood
— I'll circle back in Q1. Want me to check in then, or would you rather
I stay out?"
```

**Why this works:** it relieves pressure; gives them an out; leaves door open; respects their attention — which dramatically increases reply rates (often they reply even if declining).

## Follow-Up Rules

1. Every follow-up adds a new insight or observation — never "just bumping"
2. Max 3 touches over 9 days, then stop (matches 39.6.1)
3. Never follow up on weekends or holidays
4. Reference their world, never your need for money
5. If they say "not now", log it and return in 90 days with real value
