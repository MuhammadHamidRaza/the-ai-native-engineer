**What this solves:** Projects don't usually fail on technical merit — they fail on communication. These 12 rules keep clients calm, informed, and paying — even when things go wrong.

## The 12 Rules

1. **Over-communicate.** Weekly 5-line status every Friday, even if nothing changed.
2. **Never surprise the client.** Bad news travels fast with a plan; silence breeds panic.
3. **One channel per client.** Slack for async, Loom for walkthroughs, email for contracts.
4. **Respond within business hours.** Same-day for $50k+ engagements; 24h for the rest.
5. **Write decisions down.** "We agreed to X" in Slack/email > memory.
6. **Milestone demos, not just PRs.** They see working software weekly.
7. **Scope lock before build.** Written agreement on what's in/out — signed before code.
8. **Flag delays immediately.** Day 1 of a slip, not day 14.
9. **Say "I don't know" when true.** Then find out — trust beats omniscience.
10. **Respect their time.** Looms ≤5 min; meetings ≤45 min; agendas sent in advance.
11. **Celebrate wins publicly.** Share credit with their team; it buys loyalty.
12. **Know when to shut up.** Sometimes the best communication is letting the work speak.

## The Weekly Status Template (Copy-Paste)

```text
Status Update — Week of [Date]

Done:
- [shipped thing] — link
- [decided thing] — noted in #decisions

In progress:
- [next thing] — on track / blocked on [X]

Needs from you:
- [one decision, one date]

Risks:
- [one honest risk, one sentence]

Next milestone: [date, deliverable]
```

## When Things Go Wrong

**Structure:** What happened → What we're doing → When we'll know we're fixed → What we changed so it doesn't recur.

```text
"Alice — heads up: the staging deploy showed a memory leak under load.
We've rolled back to v0.9 and are diagnosing; ETA fix tomorrow 2pm.
No customer impact — it only hit staging. I'll update by 10am and 4pm.
Postmortem Thursday with root cause + prevention."
```

**Why this works:** honest, fast, complete. Clients forgive problems; they don't forgive surprises.
