Appendix B is the **copy-paste library** — every template, message and rule you need to execute Appendix A and Chapter 39 without staring at a blank screen. These are starting points, not scripts; personalize every send before you hit send.

## How to Use Appendix B

- **Before outreach**: read B.1–B.4 (comments, connections, first conversations, pain confirmation)
- **When they engage**: B.5–B.7 (pitches, demos, follow-ups)
- **When they're interested**: B.8–B.9 (proposals, discovery call prep)
- **Always**: B.10 (communication rules) and B.11 (20 practical outreach rules)

## The Tone Rules That Apply Everywhere

1. Write like a human, not a vendor
2. Specific > generic; numbers > adjectives
3. One idea per message
4. End with one question, never two
5. No attachments in cold outreach
6. No "just checking in" — ever
7. Proof before promise
