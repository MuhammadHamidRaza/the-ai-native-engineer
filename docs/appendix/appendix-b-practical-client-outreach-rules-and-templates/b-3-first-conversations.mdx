**What this solves:** They accepted your connection or replied to a comment. Now you get 60 seconds to deepen the relationship — not pitch, not demo, not close. Just a genuine conversation that reveals whether you two fit.

## The First-Message Formula

```text
[Reference their world — specific]
[One question — open, short]
```

**Examples:**

```text
"Hey Alice — saw you mentioned the webhook retry storm in your postmortem.
Curious: how are you alerting on it post-fix?"
```

```text
"Ben — your post about hiring for the ops role resonated. What's the #1
thing that frustrates you most about your current tooling?"
```

```text
"Carol — noticed your checkout p95 dropped after the May fix. What was
the actual culprit — connection pool or query?"
```

## What First Messages Never Contain

- A pitch or calendar link
- A case study attachment
- Multiple questions
- "Just wanted to connect"
- A 500-word essay about yourself

## The 3-Question Rule

Three messages max before you either deepen interest or disengage:

1. Your reference + one question
2. Their answer → you add one insight (not a pivot to selling)
3. One follow-up question tying their pain to your world

If they engage twice, earn the right to ask about a call. If they don't, thank them and move on.

## The Transition to Call

```text
"This is exactly the kind of problem I solve — I helped a Shopify store
kill the same 401 storm with idempotency keys + a retry backoff matrix.
Would a 15-min chat be useful, or should I just send you the postmortem
I wrote on it?"
```

**Why this works:** references your proof casually; offers two paths, one zero-cost; no pressure; easy to say yes to either.
