**What this solves:** Commenting is the cheapest warm-up that exists — zero cost, permanent proof of expertise, visible to their entire network. This section teaches you to comment in ways that make buyers notice you.

## The Anatomy of a Magnetic Comment

```text
[Observation — what you heard/saw, with a number]
[Insight — the thing they didn't say, with proof]
[Question — one open question, their world]
```

**Example:**

```text
"You mentioned p95 hit 2.8s after the May deploy. When I traced the same
pattern at ShopCore, the culprit was a blocking Redis call inside the
checkout middleware — connection pool was exhausted at ~400 concurrent
users. Curious: did you profile the connection pool, or just the query?"
```

**Why it works:** demonstrates depth, adds value to the author, and asks a question that requires real thought to answer — they will reply.

## Comments That Never Work

| Comment | Why it fails |
| :-- | :-- |
| "Great post!" | Zero signal; ignored |
| "Agree!" | Adds nothing |
| "I'd love to chat about this" | Pitching in disguise; ignored |
| "My tool can help" | Instant block |
| The same comment twice | Spam flag |

## The 5-Minute Comment Routine

1. Open LinkedIn, filter notifications for your target accounts
2. Read the post fully — 30 seconds
3. Write 2–3 sentences with one number/one insight — 2 minutes
4. Post, engage with 2 replies, move on — 2 minutes
5. **Do this daily** for 20 targets = warm pipeline in 30 days

## Comment vs Reply

- **Comment** — public, builds credibility with their audience
- **Reply to their reply** — deepen the thread, show you read
- **Direct message** — only after 2+ public exchanges

## Frequency Rules

- Max 3 comments/day per target account
- Never comment twice on one post
- Mix praise (rarely), addition, and challenge (sparingly, respectfully)
