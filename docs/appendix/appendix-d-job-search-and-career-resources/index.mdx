Appendix D is the **job-search operations manual** — checklists, audit criteria, interview frameworks, and negotiation notes that pair with Chapter 38's narrative lessons. Use it when you're actively searching, not as passive reading.

> **Scope note:** US/EU market conventions, illustrative figures (see D.4.1.1) — re-verify local salary bands and norms.

## How to Use Appendix D

- **Before applying**: D.1 (resume audit) and D.2 (GitHub/portfolio audit)
- **Before interviews**: D.3 (frameworks for coding, design, behavioral rounds)
- **Before offers**: D.4 (evaluation and negotiation notes)
- **After getting one**: D.1–D.2 again — update your materials with the new win
