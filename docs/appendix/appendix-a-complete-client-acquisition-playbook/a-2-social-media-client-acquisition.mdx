**What this solves:** You know platforms exist but posting into the void and hoping buyers find you. This playbook turns social media into a repeatable 2–4 hour/week channel — not a full-time job.

## The 80/20 of Social Client Acquisition

1. **LinkedIn is your homepage** — most B2B technical deals close here or from its referrals. Optimize it first: headline = positioning statement, banner = offer, featured = case study.
2. **One platform mastered beats five dabbled in** — pick one, post 2–3×/week for 90 days.
3. **Value first, pitch never** — every post teaches something. DMs only after engagement.

## The Weekly Cadence (3 hours)

| Day | Action | Time |
| :-- | :-- | --: |
| Mon | 1 insight post (what broke + how fixed) | 45 min |
| Wed | 1 authoritative deep-dive (methodology) | 60 min |
| Fri | 1 proof post (client win, before/after) | 30 min |
| Continuous | Comment on 10 buyers' posts, add insight | 30 min/day |

## Platform Quick-Start (Full detail in Chapter 39.4)

- **LinkedIn** — buyers, buyers, buyers. Write like an engineer who respects their time.
- **X/Twitter** — builders and indie hackers; high velocity, low trust unless you show work.
- **Reddit** — trust through contribution; r/forhire, niche subs; never pitch, never spam.
- **YouTube** — highest-trust asset; 1 video = 10k words of credibility; do 5/year minimum.

## The DM Formula That Doesn't Get Ignored

```text
Subject: [their company] — one observation, one question

Hi [Name], saw [specific trigger — their blog post, hiring ad, launch].
Noticed [observation with number]. Question: [one specific open question]?

No pitch — I work with [niche] on [outcome] and was curious how you're
thinking about [problem] today.
```

**Rules:** reference something they published; ask one question; never link to your calendar in the first message; follow up once after 4-5 business days (or twice over 9 days — see 39.6.1 / B.7), then stop.

## Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Healthy | Poor |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Profile views/week | 50+ | &lt;10 |
| Connection requests accepted | >40% | &lt;20% |
| DM reply rate | >15% | &lt;5% |
| Discovery calls/month from social | 3–5 | 0 |
