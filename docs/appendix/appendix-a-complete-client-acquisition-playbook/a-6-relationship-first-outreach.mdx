**What this solves:** Cold outreach typically converts at low single-digit percentages; relationship-first outreach converts far better. These are practitioner heuristics, not measured statistics. The difference is trust built before the ask — not faster typing.

## The Warm-Up Ladder

```text
Stage 1: Observe (Day 1) — like/comment on their content, no ask
Stage 2: Engage (Day 2–4) — reply to their post with insight, not flattery
Stage 3: Share (Day 4–6) — send a relevant resource with no pitch
Stage 4: Ask (Day 7–10) — ask one specific question tied to their world
Stage 5: Offer (Day 10+) — only if they engage, offer a low-risk pilot
```

**Golden rule:** Never skip a rung. Pitching at Stage 1–2 reads as manipulation; asking at Stage 3–4 reads as curiosity.

## The Engagement Comment That Works

Weak comment: "Great post!"
Strong comment: "You mentioned the 401 spike — I saw the same pattern when ShopCore migrated to webhook v3; the fix was idempotency keys + signature verification order. Curious if you've measured the retry storm impact?"

**Why it works:** shows technical depth, asks a question, invites dialogue — not compliance.

## The Resource DM (No Pitch)

```text
Hi [Name] — saw your thread on [topic]. Sent you a resource that
might help: [link]. It covers [specific thing] with actual numbers.

No ask — just thought of you.
```

**Why this works:** giving without asking creates reciprocity; the recipient now owes you nothing but feels fine replying.

## The Trust Accelerator: Public Proof

Before outreach, do one public thing per target: comment on their post, answer their question, share their content. When your name shows up, they recognize it — you're already warm.

## Follow-Up Discipline

- If they replied → answer substantively, then ask the low-cost question
- If they didn't reply → wait 5 business days, then stop. Never chase
- If they asked a question → answer it fully; do not pivot to selling
- If they expressed pain → only then propose a 15-minute call

## The Relationship Scorecard

Track every prospect:

| Name | Engaged? | Shared resource? | Replied? | Call booked? |
| :-- | :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| Alice @ BrightCo | Y | Y | Y | Y |
| Ben @ ShopDirect | Y | N | Y | N |

Relationships compound; scripts don't.
