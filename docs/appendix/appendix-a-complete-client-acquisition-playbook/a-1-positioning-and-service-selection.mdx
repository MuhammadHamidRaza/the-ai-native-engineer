**What this solves:** You can build anything but you don't know what to sell, to whom. Positioning is the decision that makes every subsequent sales conversation shorter, warmer, and more profitable.

## The Positioning Canvas

```text
I help [who] solve [specific pain] via [your method] so they get [measurable outcome].
```

**Example (good):** "I help Shopify stores recover abandoned checkouts with AI-driven email flows so they lift conversion 15–25% and keep $3–8k/month they were losing."

**Anti-example (bad):** "I build full-stack web apps." — no buyer, no pain, no outcome. Commodity pricing forever.

## Service Selection Decision Tree

1. **Pick 1 niche** (e-com, SaaS, local services, healthcare, logistics)
2. **Pick 1–2 outcomes** you deliver (conversion rate, cost reduction, compliance, speed-to-launch)
3. **Pick your delivery mode** (fixed sprint, retainer, per-project)
4. **Write the Statement of Outcome** (Chapter 39.1.2)
5. **Kill the rest** — everything else is a distraction until this one works

## Positioning Audit Checklist

- [ ] One sentence says who, what pain, what outcome
- [ ] One buyer persona described (title, company size, budget)
- [ ] One case study or demo from that exact niche
- [ ] Your LinkedIn/Upwork/portfolio all say the same thing
- [ ] You can name 5 people currently in your chosen niche
