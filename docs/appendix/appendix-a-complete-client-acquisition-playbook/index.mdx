## The 30-60-90 Day Client Acquisition Plan

```text
Days 1–30:  Foundation — ICP defined, 2 packages written, 1 case study live,
            50 targeted prospects built, outreach starts
Days 31–60: Pipeline — 3–5 discovery calls/week, 1–2 proposals/week,
            iterate on messaging, close first 1–2 clients
Days 61–90: Compounding — deliver, ask for referrals, publish 1 proof asset/mo,
            raise rates 15–20%, build retainer offer
```

## Reality Check (No Hype)

- Months 1–3 are the hardest. Expect the majority of your time on sales, not delivery.
- Most solo engineers land their first paid client from someone they already know — warm network beats cold every time.
- Experienced independent engineers commonly reach solid mid–five-to-six-figure annual revenue once established (varies widely by niche, market and hours). This is directional practitioner experience, not a guarantee — treat it as a ceiling hint, not an expectation.
- Churn is normal in independent consulting; plan for it and treat lost clients as feedback, not failure.
- You do not need an audience. You need 30 people who trust you with their budget.
