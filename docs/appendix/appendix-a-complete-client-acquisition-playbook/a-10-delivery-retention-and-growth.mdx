**What this solves:** You closed one client. Now the real game begins: delivering so well they refer you, retaining so well they renew, and growing so well each client becomes two.

## The Delivery Playbook

```text
Week 1 — Kickoff + audit (go deep on their actual data, not demo data)
Week 2 — Build in their environment, not yours (PR reviews, staging)
Week 3 — Iterate with their feedback daily
Week 4 — Deploy + train their team (record runbook video)
```

**Rules:** over-communicate weekly (Friday 5-line status); never surprise the client; every change goes through their PR review; you are building *their* asset, not your portfolio.

## The Retention Machine

1. **30-day post-launch check** — "How's it running? Any errors? Need a hand?" — free, 20 minutes, builds loyalty
2. **Monthly health report** — 1 page: metrics, wins, one recommendation
3. **Quarterly roadmap session** — what's next, what's out of scope, what's the budget
4. **Annual renewal conversation** — escalate existing value, not the price

## Referral Engine

Happy clients refer — but only if you ask:

```text
"Hey [Name] — glad the checkout fix is holding up. If someone comes to
mind who'd benefit from the same, I'd love an intro. I'll take good care
of them — and I'll make you look good for connecting us."
```

**Reciprocity:** refer them to your network too — it compounds.

## Growth Levers

| Lever | How |
| :-- | :-- |
| Retainers | Convert project clients to $3–5k/mo ongoing |
| Expansion | "You loved the checkout work — want the same for your 3 other stores?" |
| Referrals | Ask after every win; track them |
| Productize | Turn your best solution into a WooCommerce plugin/SaaS |
| Raise rates | +15–25% every 6 months with proof |

## The Solo Consultant's End Game

Year 1: 2–3 clients, $60–90k, figuring out delivery
Year 2: 4–5 clients, $120–180k, referrals flowing
Year 3: niche authority, $200k+, productized offering, subcontract overflow

You don't need 50 clients. You need 5 good ones who trust you and refer reliably.
