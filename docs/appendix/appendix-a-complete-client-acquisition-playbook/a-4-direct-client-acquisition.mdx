**What this solves:** Cold email and DMs feel spammy. Done right, direct acquisition is the highest-converting channel for technical services — because you're reaching people who already have the pain.

## The Research → Personalize → Ask Sequence

1. **Target** — 20 companies, not 200. Quality beats volume every time.
2. **Research 5 minutes each** — site, blog, recent posts, stack (Wappalyzer), team size
3. **Personalize** — one specific observation with numbers
4. **Ask** — one low-cost question, not "hire me"
5. **Follow up twice, then stop** — after 4 and 9 business days (matching 39.6.1 / B.7)

## The Cold Email That Gets Replies

```text
Subject: [Company] — one observation about [specific thing]

Hi [First],

Saw [specific trigger: their blog, job ad, launch, funding].
Noticed [observation — e.g., "your checkout has 3 seconds of blocking JS;
that's 2× the Shopify benchmark"].

Question: are you tracking [metric] today?

No pitch — I work with [niche] on [outcome]. Curious how you're
thinking about [problem] as you scale.

[Your name]
```

**Why this works:** it shows you did work before asking for their time; it asks a question a busy person can answer in 15 seconds; it positions you as a peer, not a vendor.

## Volume Math

- 20 researched emails/week → 4–6 replies → 2 discovery calls → 1 proposal → 1 close every 4–6 weeks
- 20-30% reply rate is workable; &lt;10% means your targeting or personalization is off (practitioner heuristics, not benchmarks)

## Warm-Up Sequence (Preferred)

1. Like/comment on their post (Day 1)
2. Follow up with a relevant resource DM (Day 3–5)
3. Ask the question (Day 7–10)

**Rule:** Cold emailing someone who has never seen your name can work — but expect materially lower reply rates than after a week of light public engagement (a comment or two). Research first, warm up when you can, and never blast. (Practitioner heuristic — not a measured statistic.)

## Stop Rules

- Client asks for free work → decline politely
- They want to skip discovery → insist on it (scope creep is guaranteed otherwise)
- Budget below your floor → refer out
- They disrespect your process → walk
