**What this solves:** You can't sell to a pain you can't see. Most engineers guess at what hurts businesses — then pitch solutions no one asked for. This playbook teaches you to diagnose pain like a consultant.

## The Pain Discovery Framework

Every business pain is one of five types:

1. **Revenue loss** — abandoned carts, churn, lost leads, slow support
2. **Cost excess** — cloud bills, manual data entry, tooling sprawl
3. **Speed drag** — slow deploys, manual processes, review bottlenecks
4. **Risk exposure** — compliance gaps, security incidents, data loss
5. **Quality decay** — bugs reaching customers, inconsistent output

## The Symptom → Root Cause Ladder

```text
Symptom: "Customers complain about slowness"
  ↓
Surface cause: "API latency p95 = 2.8s"
  ↓
Root cause: "N+1 queries + no connection pooling on the checkout service"
  ↓
Business impact: "12% cart abandonment × $85 AOV × 1,200 orders/mo = $12k/mo lost"
```

When you can say the last line out loud to a buyer, the sale is 80% done.

## Research Toolkit (30 minutes per target)

- **Company site** — /about, /careers (hiring pain), /blog (their priorities)
- **Wappalyzer** — stack, hosting, analytics, email tooling
- **BuiltWith** — same, deeper; traffic estimates
- **Google reviews / G2 / Trustpilot** — literal customer pain, quoted
- **LinkedIn** — who's hiring, who just joined, who left
- **Job posts** — the "ideal candidate" section lists their pains verbatim
- **Support pages** — "known issues" sections are gold

## The Pain Map Template

```text
Company: ______
Trigger: [blog post / job ad / funding / launch / outage]
Observed symptom: ______
Suspected root cause: ______
Business cost estimate: $______/month
My fix: ______
Time to value: ______ weeks
```

## How to Talk About Pain Without Sounding Like a Vendor

| Don't say | Say |
| :-- | :-- |
| "I can optimize your site" | "I saw your checkout posts 401s in Shopify's webhook logs — did you know?" |
| "You need AI" | "Your support team is 5 people handling 800 tickets/month — want me to show you what 40% deflection looks like?" |
| "Let me build you a dashboard" | "Your ops team spends Fridays in spreadsheets — I measured the actual cost: $14k/yr in someone's salary doing Excel" |
