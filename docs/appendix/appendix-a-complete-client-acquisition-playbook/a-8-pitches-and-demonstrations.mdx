**What this solves:** You've had the discovery call. Now you need to show, not tell — and close the gap between "interested" and "signed".

## The Demo That Closes

Structure (15 minutes max):

```text
1. Restate their pain (30s) — "You told me webhook failures cost $12k/mo."
2. Show the fix, live (10 min) — real code, real data, their stack
3. Show the metric (2 min) — "Here's the dashboard that catches it"
4. Scope & price (2 min) — "4 weeks, $18k, includes deployment + training"
5. Ask (1 min) — "Shall we move forward, or is there a question I can answer?"
```

**Why live demos win:** screenshots lie; working code doesn't. A buyer who watches the fix work has already imagined it in production — that's the sale.

## The Prototype-First Close

Instead of a demo, send a **micro-prototype** the day after discovery:

- One endpoint that does the thing
- One dashboard widget
- One email they receive from "their" system

**Time budget:** 2–4 hours. **Cost to you:** low. **Value to them:** "oh, this actually works" — price becomes irrelevant.

## Pitch Framing for Non-Technical Buyers

| Don't lead with | Lead with |
| :-- | :-- |
| "It uses Stripe webhooks and idempotency keys" | "You will never lose a payment silently again" |
| "It's built on pgvector + OpenAI" | "Your support team deflects 40% of tickets automatically" |
| "It scales to 10k RPS" | "Black Friday won't break your checkout" |

**Rule:** technology is your résumé; outcome is their decision.

## The One-Page Proposal That Closes

```text
Client: ______
Problem (their words): ______
Outcome (measurable): ______
Scope: 3 milestones with dates
Investment: $X (50% upfront, 50% on delivery)
Terms: Net 14, IP transfers on final payment
Timeline: ______
Risk: what could delay us (and how we mitigate)
```

**Send within 24 hours of the demo.** Speed signals competence.

## Handling "It's Too Expensive"

Never defend the number. Return to their pain: "What's the cost of another 3 months of $12k/mo leaks?" — then let them do the math. If they still balk, offer a smaller pilot, not a discount.
