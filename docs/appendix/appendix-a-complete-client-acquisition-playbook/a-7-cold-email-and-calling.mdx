**What this solves:** You need appointments with people who don't know you. Cold outreach is a numbers game that most people play badly — too much volume, too little personalization, too fast to the ask.

> **Compliance box (US/EU — read first):** Before you email anyone, know that US **CAN-SPAM** requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email, a working opt-out ("Reply STOP") that is honored within 10 business days, and no misleading subject/headers. The EU/UK **GDPR/ePrivacy** regime is stricter: B2B email is lawful under "legitimate interests" in many member states, but includes transparency, an easy opt-out from the first touch, and deletion on request; violations carry real fines. **Never buy scraped lists, never hide who you are, honor every opt-out immediately, and log consent.** This page is engineering guidance, not legal advice — if you do real outreach, spend 30 minutes with the FTC's CAN-SPAM guide and your local DPA guidance before the first send.

## Cold Email — The 3-Part Formula That Works

```text
Subject: [Company] — one observation about [specific thing]

Hi [First],

[Specific observation — 1 sentence, with a number or artifact].
[Question — 1 sentence].
No pitch, no calendar link, no "just checking in".

[Your name] · [one-line credential]
```

**Example:**

```text
Subject: BrightCo — your checkout 401s

Hi Alice,

Your Shopify webhook logs show a 12% 401 rate on cart_created since the
v3 migration — that's revenue leaking silently.

Question: are you alerting on webhook failures today?

[Your name] — I help Shopify stores fix webhook + idempotency issues
```

## Deliverability Before Copy

- Domain warmup 2–4 weeks (use Instantly/Smartlead or Google Workspace with low volume)
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC configured — check `mail-tester.com` score > 9/10
- Send from a subdomain, not your primary
- 20–30 personalized sends/day per mailbox (after a 2–4 week warm-up)
- Never buy lists — build them with Apollo/LinkedIn Sales Navigator

## Cold Calling — The 60-Second Script

```text
"Hi [Name], this is [Your name] — I work with Shopify merchants on checkout
reliability. Do you have 30 seconds?

[If yes] I noticed your checkout abandonment is 18% — I'd bet half of that
is a technical trigger. Is that a metric you watch today?

[If no] Fair enough. What's the best way to reach you? Email, or should I
try again tomorrow at 2pm?"
```

**Rules:** know their stack before calling; never read a script; if they say no twice, thank them and move on; leave a voicemail ≤20s.

## Follow-Up Cadence

| Touch | Channel | Content |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Day 1 | Email | Observation + question |
| Day 3 | Email | One case study, 100 words |
| Day 7 | Email | Last attempt, one sentence + "don't let me bother you" |
| Day 14 | LinkedIn | Connection + brief note |

**Stop rules:** no reply after 3 emails + 1 call = move on. Do not chase.

## Metrics

- Contacted → replied: 10–15% (warm-up first: 25–35%)
- Replied → discovery call: 40–50%
- Call → proposal: 30–40%
- Proposal → close: 30–40%

If your numbers are &lt;5% reply rate, your targeting or personalization is broken — not the medium.
