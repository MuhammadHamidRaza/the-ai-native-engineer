Appendix C is the **practice gym** — 40+ structured exercises that build the skills Chapter 39 teaches and Appendices A–B document. No theory, no filler: work these in order, track your numbers, and graduate with a real pipeline.

## How to Use Appendix C

- **Complete one exercise per day for 40 days** — mastery beats intensity
- **Track in a spreadsheet** — opens, replies, calls, proposals, closes
- **Bring your output to each session** — feedback is the curriculum
- **Do not skip the role-play exercises** — they build the muscle you need live

## The 40-Day Progression

```text
Days 1–7:    Niche research + business research (Exercises 1–10)
Days 8–14:   Pain-point identification + lead qualification (Exercises 11–20)
Days 15–21:  Outreach writing + demo planning (Exercises 21–30)
Days 22–35:  Sales role-play + pipeline management (Exercises 31–38)
Days 36–40:  Capstone assignment — full client hunt, start to finish
```
