**Purpose:** Before any outreach, become the expert on your 10 target companies — their stack, their pain, their timing, their buyer.

## Exercise 1 — Stack Reconnaissance (30 min per company)

For each of your 10 targets, run:

1. **Wappalyzer** — frontend, backend, hosting, analytics, email tooling
2. **BuiltWith** — traffic estimate, ad spend, tech adoption timeline
3. **Their blog/careers page** — priorities, hiring pain, recent wins
4. **LinkedIn** — team size, who joined recently, who left
5. **Google reviews** — literal customer complaints, verbatim

**Deliverable:** one-page dossier per company: stack summary, pain hypothesis, contact, trigger event.

## Exercise 2 — Trigger Event Hunt (20 min per company)

Find one **trigger event** — something that makes now the right time:

- New funding round
- New VP Engineering hired
- New product launch
- Public outage/incident postmortem
- Job post mentioning the exact pain you solve
- Competitor launched something they can't match

**Deliverable:** 10 trigger events, one per company. No trigger = deprioritize.

## Exercise 3 — Buyer Mapping (30 min per company)

Identify the **real buyer**, not the loudest complainer:

- Who owns the budget for this problem?
- Who feels the pain daily?
- Who would veto your proposal, and why?
- Who would champion you internally?

**Deliverable:** 2–3 names per company with roles and relationships.

## Exercise 4 — Competitive Research (45 min total)

For your niche, find the top 3 competing solutions/freelancers.

**Deliverable:**

- Their positioning
- Their pricing
- Their weakness (what they ignore)
- Your counter-positioning

## Exercise 5 — The 10-Minute Research Sprint (timed)

Pick one company you know nothing about. In 10 minutes, find:

1. What they do
2. What their stack is
3. One pain signal
4. One trigger event
5. One decision-maker

**Deliverable:** the 5 facts, verified, in writing. Practice until you can do this cold in under 10 minutes.

## Scoring

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: |
| Stack accuracy | Guessing | Mostly right | Verified, detailed |
| Pain hypothesis | Vague | Specific | Quantified ($ or time) |
| Trigger event | None | One found | Strong catalyst with date |
| Buyer map | Contact unknown | Role known | Name + relationship understood |

**Target: ≥16/20 before outreach.**
