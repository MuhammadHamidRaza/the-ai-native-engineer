**Purpose:** Execute the full cycle end-to-end — from zero pipeline to a signed client (or a definitive pass) — in 30 days. This is the capstone of the workbook; everything before leads here.

## The Assignment

**Deliverables (submit all 5):**

1. **Niche scorecard** — from C.1, one niche picked, scored ≥16/20
2. **10 target dossiers** — from C.2, one page each
3. **Outreach sequence** — 3 sent messages with replies logged
4. **Discovery call** — one completed call (live or role-play), with notes
5. **Verdict** — one paragraph: pursue / nurture / disqualify, and why

## The 30-Day Timeline

| Week | Focus | Milestone |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Niche + research | 10 dossiers complete |
| 2 | Outreach | 10 messages sent, replies logged |
| 3 | Follow-up + role-play | 1 call booked or 5 role-plays done |
| 4 | Close or disqualify | Verdict written, pipeline updated |

## The Final Rubric (100 points)

| Area | Weight | What Excellent Looks Like |
| :-- | --: | :-- |
| Niche selection | 10 | Specific, moneyed, repeatable |
| Research quality | 20 | Pain quantified; trigger identified; buyer mapped |
| Outreach | 20 | Personal, specific, outcome-focused; ≥15% reply rate |
| Discovery | 20 | Listened > spoke; quantified pain; clear next step |
| Judgment | 30 | Ruthless disqualification OR evidence-based pursuit |

**Passing:** ≥70. **Distinction:** ≥85 with a signed engagement letter or a written "pass" with full reasoning.

## What You're Really Being Tested On

Not charm. Not charisma. Not "networking". The test is: **can you do the work, find the people who need it, and have an honest conversation about money and outcomes?** That's the whole job. This assignment proves you can.

## If You Don't Land a Client in 30 Days

That's fine — most people don't their first time out. What matters:

- Did you maintain the pipeline discipline?
- Did you learn what broke (targeting? message? discovery? pricing?)
- Can you name the one thing you'll change next attempt?

If yes, you have done what most engineers only say they want to do. If no, you have 39 more weeks to practice here.
