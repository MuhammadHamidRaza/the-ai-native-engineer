**Purpose:** Not every interested prospect deserves your time. Qualification is the discipline of only pursuing deals you can win, at a price you want, on a timeline that works.

## Exercise 1 — The BANT Scorecard (20 min per lead)

Score each prospect 1–5 on:

- **Budget** — do they have it or can get it?
- **Authority** — are you speaking to the decision-maker?
- **Need** — is the pain acute and acknowledged?
- **Timeline** — when do they want it solved?

| Lead | Budget | Authority | Need | Timeline | Total | Pursue? |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: | :--: |
| Alice | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 18 | Yes |

**Threshold:** ≥14 = pursue. 10–13 = nurture 30 days. &lt;10 = disqualify.

## Exercise 2 — The 5-Minute Qualification Call (role-play)

Practice on a partner (or solo, record):

```text
Minute 1 — "What prompted this conversation?"
Minute 2 — "What's it costing you per month?"
Minute 3 — "Who else is involved in this decision?"
Minute 4 — "What's your timeline?"
Minute 5 — "What would make this a clear yes for you?"
```

**Deliverable:** transcript + notes. Did you get 4/5 answers?

## Exercise 3 — The Disqualification Drill (30 min)

Write 5 polite disqualification lines you'd say to:

1. A prospect with no budget
2. A prospect who wants free work "to prove yourself"
3. A prospect who wants to skip discovery
4. A prospect whose timeline is "ASAP" with no scope
5. A prospect who disrespects your process

**Deliverable:** 5 lines, toned firm but kind.

## Exercise 5 — The Weekly Review (every Friday, 15 min)

Every Friday:

- Which leads advanced? Why?
- Which stalled? Why?
- What will you do differently next week?

**Deliverable:** 3 bullets in a journal, every Friday for 10 weeks.

## Scoring

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: |
| Budget clarity | Vague | Estimated | Confirmed or approvable |
| Decision-maker | Unknown | Known | Engaged |
| Timeline | Someday | This quarter | This month |
| Disqualification | Can't say no | Sometimes | Ruthless, respectful |

**Target: ≥16/20 per lead.**
