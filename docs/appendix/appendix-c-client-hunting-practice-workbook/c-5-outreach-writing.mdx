**Purpose:** Turn research into outreach that gets replies — specific, human, outcome-focused, never spammy.

## Exercise 1 — The 100-Word Constraint (45 min)

Write 10 different cold emails for your chosen niche. Each must:

- Open with a specific observation (with a number)
- Include one genuine question
- Stay under 100 words
- Never mention "I" more than twice

**Deliverable:** 10 emails, each &lt;100 words, each unique.

## Exercise 2 — The Subject Line Test (20 min)

For each email, write 3 subject lines. Rank by click potential.

| Email | Subject A | Subject B | Subject C | Winner |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | "Checkout 401s" | "Your Shopify webhook" | "Quick question" | B |

**Deliverable:** 10 emails × 3 subjects = 30 lines, ranked.

## Exercise 3 — The Warm-Up Sequence (30 min)

For one target company, write the full 5-touch warm-up:

1. Day 1 — Comment on their post (write the actual comment)
2. Day 3 — Share a relevant resource (write the DM)
3. Day 5 — Like + reply to their reply
4. Day 7 — Ask your question (write the email)
5. Day 10 — Follow up or disengage (write both)

**Deliverable:** the 5-touch plan, fully scripted.

## Exercise 4 — The DM vs Email Decision (20 min)

For each of your 10 targets, decide channel:

- If they post on LinkedIn daily → DM or comment
- If they're quiet on social but active in email → email
- If they're a GitHub/open-source maintainer → GitHub issue or email

**Deliverable:** 10 channel decisions with one-line rationale.

## Exercise 5 — The Reply Mastery Drill (45 min)

Practice responding to 5 different reply types:

1. "Not interested" → graceful close + door open
2. "Tell me more" → send one-page spec
3. "How much?" → outcome-first answer, not number
4. "We're evaluating options" → differentiation, not pressure
5. "Budget is tight" → smaller audit or pilot

**Deliverable:** 5 scripted responses, toned warm and professional.

## Scoring

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: |
| Specificity | Generic | Somewhat specific | Unmistakably about them |
| Brevity | 200+ words | ~120 words | Under 100 |
| Question quality | None | Yes/no | Open, insightful |
| Channel fit | Wrong channel | Okay | Perfect fit |

**Target: ≥16/20 on your best email.**
