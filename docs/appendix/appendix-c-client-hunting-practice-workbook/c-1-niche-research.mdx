**Purpose:** Pick a niche with money, pain, and a repeatable engagement model — not a passion project that goes nowhere.

## Exercise 1 — Market Sizing Sprint (90 min)

Pick **three** candidate niches (e.g., Shopify stores >$1M/yr, B2B SaaS onboarding, dental clinics going digital).

For each, answer:

1. How many companies exist in this niche? (Google "how many [X] in US")
2. What's their average annual revenue?
3. Do they already pay for technical services? (Upwork job search, Clutch directory)
4. What's the #1 technical pain they post about publicly?
5. What would they pay for a fix? (estimate from job posts or forum mentions)

**Deliverable:** one-page scorecard per niche. Pick the winner.

## Exercise 2 — Willingness-to-Pay Test (60 min)

Find 5 real companies in your chosen niche. For each:

1. Find one public pain signal (blog, job ad, review, forum)
2. Estimate what they'd pay to fix it
3. Write the outreach email you'd send (don't send yet — this is research)

**Deliverable:** table of 5 companies × pain × estimated budget × outreach line.

## Exercise 3 — Competitive Audit (45 min)

Find 3 competitors/freelancers serving this niche.

**Deliverable:**

- What do they charge?
- What do they promise?
- What gap exists that you can fill?
- What's your unfair advantage?

## Exercise 4 — Niche Positioning Statement (30 min)

Write your one-sentence positioning:

```text
I help [niche] solve [specific pain] via [your method] so they get [outcome].
```

Test it on 2 people outside tech. If they can't repeat it back, rewrite.

## Exercise 5 — The 10-Company Shortlist (60 min)

Build your target list of 10 companies. For each:

| Company | Pain signal | Contact | Budget est. | Status |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | --: | :--: |
| AcmeCo | "Checkout 401s cost us $12k/mo" | CTO @ LinkedIn | $15–25k | To contact |

**Deliverable:** the 10-row table filled in, ready for outreach.

## Scoring

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: |
| Niche clarity | Vague | Somewhat specific | Sharply defined, one sentence |
| Willingness to pay | Unknown | Some evidence | Clear budget signals |
| Repeatability | One-off | Occasional repeats | Standardizable engagement |
| Your edge | None | Some | Clear, defensible advantage |

**You need ≥16/20 to proceed to C.2.** Below that, pick a different niche.
