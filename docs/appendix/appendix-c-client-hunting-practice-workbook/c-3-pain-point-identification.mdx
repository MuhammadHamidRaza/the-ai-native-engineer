**Purpose:** Convert vague frustration into a quantified, ownable business problem — the kind a buyer will pay to make disappear.

## Exercise 1 — The Pain Interview Simulator (role-play)

With a partner (or solo, record it), practice the discovery flow:

1. **Warm open** — "Thanks for the 15 minutes. Walk me through how you handle [process] today."
2. **Clarify** — "What does that look like on a bad day?"
3. **Quantify** — "How often? What does that cost you — time, money, customers?"
4. **Validate** — "If this stayed broken for another year, what happens?"
5. **Close** — "What would success look like for you in 90 days?"

**Deliverable:** 10-minute recorded role-play + transcript (or notes). Review: did you ask more than you spoke?

## Exercise 2 — The Pain Ladder (30 min per company)

For each of your 10 targets, climb the ladder:

```text
Surface symptom: "slow checkout"
  ↓
Concrete metric: "p95 = 2.8s; 12% abandonment"
  ↓
Root cause: "N+1 Shopify API calls in cart middleware"
  ↓
Business cost: "$12,240/month lost revenue at 1,200 orders x $85 AOV x 12% (1,200 x $85 x 0.12 = $12,240)"
  ↓
Your fix: "Batch API calls + Redis cache; p95 → 400ms; abandonment → 7%"
```

**Deliverable:** one ladder per target company.

## Exercise 3 — The Business Cost Estimator (45 min)

For each pain, build the spreadsheet:

| Input | Value |
| :-- | --: |
| Frequency | X per month |
| Cost per instance | $Y |
| Monthly impact | $X × $Y |
| Annual impact | $X × $Y × 12 |
| Your fee (15–25% of annual impact) | $Z |

**Deliverable:** 10 spreadsheets (or one master). You must be able to say the number out loud in one sentence.

## Exercise 4 — The "So What?" Test (15 min)

For your top 3 companies, write one sentence that answers:

```text
"This costs you $___/month and here's why you should care today."
```

**Deliverable:** 3 killer sentences, each &lt;20 words, each with a number.

## Exercise 5 — Disqualification Practice (20 min)

Practice saying no. For each of your 10 targets, ask:

- Is the pain urgent? (if no → disqualify)
- Is there budget? (if no → nurture, don't pitch)
- Can I fix it in my stack? (if no → refer out)
- Do they trust me enough to give me access? (if no → build trust first)

**Deliverable:** 10 dispositions: 3 pursue aggressively, 4 nurture, 3 disqualify politely.

## Scoring

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: |
| Pain clarity | Vague | Specific | Quantified with $/time |
| Root cause | Guessing | Plausible | Verified or clearly evidenced |
| Business case | "Would be nice" | Clear cost | $/month + ROI on your fee |
| Disqualification | Can't say no | Sometimes | Ruthless, with reasons |

**Target: ≥16/20 before moving to outreach.**
