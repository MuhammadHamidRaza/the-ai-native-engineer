**Purpose:** Turn 15 minutes of their time into a qualified opportunity — through a demo that makes the problem obvious and the solution inevitable.

## Exercise 1 — The 15-Minute Demo Script (60 min)

Write the full script for your demo:

```text
0:00–0:30  Restate their pain (their exact words)
0:30–2:00  The fix — architecture in 90 seconds (1 slide)
2:00–10:00 Live code — build the fix against their scenario
10:00–12:30 The metric — before/after, dashboard screenshot
12:30–14:00 Scope & investment
14:00–15:00 Ask — "Shall we move forward?"
```

**Deliverable:** the full script, timed, with speaker notes.

## Exercise 2 — The One-Slide Architecture (30 min)

Design the single slide you'll show at 0:30:

- Their stack (logo row)
- The problem (one line with a number)
- The fix (architecture diagram — boxes and arrows)
- The outcome (one metric)

**Deliverable:** one slide, exportable, no jargon.

## Exercise 3 — The Live-Code Rehearsal (90 min)

Build the fix live in a clean environment:

1. Fork their scenario (test store, test repo)
2. Implement the fix under 10 minutes
3. Show one test passing
4. Show the metric moving

**Deliverable:** a recorded run-through (screen recording, ≤10 min) or live rehearsal notes.

## Exercise 4 — The Objection Prep Sheet (30 min)

Write your answers to the 5 most likely objections:

1. "How does this integrate with our existing tools?"
2. "What if it breaks in production?"
3. "How long until we see results?"
4. "What's the maintenance burden?"
5. "Why you and not our current vendor?"

**Deliverable:** 5 objection scripts, each ≤3 sentences.

## Exercise 5 — The Demo Debrief Template (15 min)

Create the email you send within 2 hours of every demo:

```text
Alice — great walking through it today. Recap:
 — Problem: [one sentence]
 — Impact: [$ number]
 — Fix: [one sentence]
 — Investment: $[X] · Timeline: [dates]
I'll send the one-page proposal tomorrow by 10am.
Anything I should adjust before then?
```

**Deliverable:** the template, saved, ready to use.

## Scoring

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: |
| Pain restatement | Your words | Mixed | Their exact words |
| Live demo | Scripted | Semi-live | Fully live, under pressure |
| Metric specificity | "Faster" | "2.5x" | "p95 2.8s → 0.4s; abandonment 12% → 7%" |
| Ask | Vague | Specific | Signed or dated next step |

**Target: ≥16/20 per demo.**
