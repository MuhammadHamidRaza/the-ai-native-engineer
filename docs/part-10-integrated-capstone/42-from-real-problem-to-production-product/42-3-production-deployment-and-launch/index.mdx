The last 20% of a capstone — the part that is the entire client experience — is where engineers cut corners because the code already "works". This section covers the production disciplines that turn a finished repo into a trusted delivery: testing pyramid, observability, hardening, and a handover the client actually uses.

## Lessons in Subchapter 42.3

1. **[42.3.1 Testing, Observability and Security Hardening](/book/part-10-integrated-capstone/42-from-real-problem-to-production-product/42-3-production-deployment-and-launch/42-3-1-testing-observability-and-security-hardening):** The practical pyramid, structured logs and the one alert that matters, and a 30-minute red-team pass against your own build.
2. **[42.3.2 Shipping to Production and Delivering Outcomes](/book/part-10-integrated-capstone/42-from-real-problem-to-production-product/42-3-production-deployment-and-launch/42-3-2-shipping-to-production-and-delivering-outcomes):** The one-page deploy/rollback runbook, the 8-item handover pack, demo protocol, and how to prove you moved the needle.

## How to Use This Subchapter

Run the red-team pass **before** your first client demo. The failure you find is the failure that would have embarrassed you in front of the person paying you.
